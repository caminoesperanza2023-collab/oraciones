Text of the prayer

In 1884, Pope Leo I1 13th finished celebrating mass and suddenly collapsed into a trance in front of stunned cardinals when he woke up pale and shaking. He said he had just heard the voice of Satan boasting to God that he could destroy the church if given enough time and power.

Terrified by what he witnessed, Pope Leo immediately composed a prayer and ordered it recited after every mass in [music] the Catholic world for decades. A direct plea to the one angel scripture says defeated Satan himself, Michael the Archangel, pray it with me now. St.

Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle. Be our protection against the wickedness and snares of the devil. May God rebuke [music] him, we humbly pray. And do thou, O prince of the heavenly host. By the power of God, cast into hell Satan and all the evil spirits who prowl about the world seeking the ruin of souls.

Amen. You just prayed the same words a pope gave the whole church after facing something he never [music] forgot. Pray this daily and walk in confidence. You are protected.

What the Church teaches

The Prayer to Saint Michael was born from something Pope Leo XIII experienced in 1884 — after Mass, he collapsed into a trance and later said he had heard Satan boasting he could destroy the Church. The Pope immediately wrote this prayer and ordered it prayed after every Mass: 'Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle, be our protection against the wickedness and snares of the devil...' Scripture shows Michael as the warrior who cast the dragon out of heaven (Revelation 12). Pray it every morning — it takes 30 seconds and puts your day under the protection of God's general.
