The beatitudes Explained: Jesus' Most Revolutionary Words on the Mount

Two thousand years ago, Jesus sat on a hillside and spoke words that turned the world upside down. The Beatitudes are a roadmap to Heaven: blessed are the poor in spirit, those who mourn, the meek, the merciful, the pure in heart, the peacemakers.

Discover the meaning of each Beatitude from the Sermon on the Mount and how they can transform your life today.

What the video says

Two thousand years ago, Jesus sat on a hillside and spoke the most revolutionary words ever heard. He began with eight blessings that turned the world's values upside down. Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven. Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.

Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth. Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled. Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God. Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God.

Blessed are those who are persecuted for righteousness, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven. These are not just words. They are a roadmap to heaven. Which Beatitude speaks to your heart today?

What the Church teaches

🙏 The Beatitudes are Jesus' roadmap to true happiness, spoken in the Sermon on the Mount. Blessed are the poor in spirit, those who mourn, the meek, those who hunger for righteousness, the merciful, the pure in heart, the peacemakers, and those persecuted for righteousness. These words turned the world's values upside down: real greatness is found not in power or riches, but in humility, mercy and a pure heart. They are not just ideals to admire, but a path to walk that leads straight to the Kingdom of Heaven. Which Beatitude speaks most to your heart today?
