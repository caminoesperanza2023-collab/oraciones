Prayer for a Difficult Decision: Lord, Show Me the Next Step

Prayer for a difficult decision: if you don't know what to do and you're afraid of getting it wrong, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and put your decision in God's hands.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Saint James says it plainly: if anyone lacks wisdom, let him ask God. And it will be given to him.

Lord, this is in front of me and I can't see the way. I'm afraid of getting it wrong. Take away my impatience and my pride. Send me someone who will give me good advice. Give me peace in my heart if this is the way, and close the door if it isn't. I'm not asking to see the whole road, only the next step. Today I put this decision in your hands.

Virgin Mary, you said yes without knowing everything: walk with me in this decision.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Saint James writes: "If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God" (James 1:5). Asking God does not replace thinking, listening or seeking good advice. It means making your choice with him, one step at a time, and we entrust it to the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism says that when a decision is hard we should look for what is right and good, helped by the virtue of prudence, the advice of competent people and the Holy Spirit and his gifts (Catechism 1787-1788). So prayer does not replace good advice: it goes with it. If the decision is a serious one, talk to a priest or to someone you trust before you choose.
