Powerful Morning Prayer to Start Your Day With God

Before the world gets hold of your day, give it to God first.

Watch the video

Text of the prayer

The saints knew this. That is why they knelt before they did anything else. How you begin your morning shapes your entire day. This is not a motivational idea. It is a spiritual truth tested by thousands of years of Christian experience. St. Thomas Moore [music] before facing execution prayed the morning office with his family.

[music] St. TZ of Lux said that her first thought every morning was to offer the day entirely to God. St. John Paul [music] II spent his first hour every morning lying face down in prayer. The saints who changed history were mourning people not because they were disciplined but because they understood that without God everything they were about to do was hollow.

Here is a simple morning prayer that can change your day. Lord, this day belongs to you. My eyes, my hands, my words, my thoughts. Take them, use them. I give you this morning and with it everything I am and everything I face today. If I am afraid, be my courage.

If I am tired, be my strength. If I am tempted, be my shield. Whatever comes today, let me meet it with you. Not ahead of you, not behind you, but with you. And at the end of this day, let me have loved someone better because I started with you.

Begin there before the phone, before the news, before the coffee. If you can manage it, give God the first moment. He will take care of the rest. Try this prayer tomorrow morning before you check anything else. Just five minutes. God will meet you there.
