Prayer to Forgive Someone: Lord, I Can't Do This Without You

Prayer to forgive someone: if someone hurt you and you can't let it go, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and give that wound to God.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Peter asked Jesus how many times he must forgive. And Jesus said: seventy times seven.

Lord, you know what they did to me. You know how much it still hurts. But I give you this wound. Take the anger that won't let me sleep. Heal what I can't heal. And help me to want what you want, even if it takes me all my life. Today, with your help, I choose to forgive.

Virgin Mary, you forgave at the foot of the cross: hold my heart.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Peter asked how many times he must forgive, and Jesus answered: "seventy times seven" (Matthew 18:22). Forgiving is not forgetting and it is not saying that what happened was fine. It is asking God for the grace you cannot find on your own, and we entrust it to the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism says that we cannot simply stop feeling a wound or forget it, but the heart that offers itself to the Holy Spirit "turns injury into compassion" (Catechism 2843). Forgiving does not mean excusing evil, and it does not mean staying in a place where you are being harmed: if you are in danger, seek help first. Forgiveness is a grace we ask for, often many times, as Jesus told Peter (Matthew 18:22).
