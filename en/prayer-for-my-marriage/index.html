Prayer for My Marriage: Lord, Bless Our Home

Prayer for my marriage: if your marriage is going through a hard time, or you simply want to protect it, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your home in the hands of Jesus.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said: what God has joined together, no human being must separate.

Lord, today I place our marriage in your hands. You were present at the wedding at Cana; fill our home again with the new wine of your love. Heal the wounds we have caused each other, take away pride and silence, and teach us to forgive before the sun goes down.

Help us look at each other with tenderness again, listen with patience, and walk together toward you.

And let's pray for one another. Amen.

What the Bible says

"What God has joined together, no human being must separate" (Matthew 19:6). Ask Jesus, who was present at the wedding at Cana, to heal the wounds, take away pride and silence, and renew the love between husband and wife.

What the Church teaches

Jesus performed his first miracle at a wedding: at Cana he turned water into wine when the celebration ran out (John 2:1-11). The Church sees this as a sign that Christ blesses marriage and sustains it when love seems to run dry (Catechism 1613). Saint Paul gives very practical advice: "Do not let the sun set on your anger" (Ephesians 4:26).
