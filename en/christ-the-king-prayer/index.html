Prayer to Jesus, King of the Universe: Reign in My Heart

Christ the king prayer: this Sunday the Church celebrates Jesus, King of the Universe. Pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let him reign in your heart, your home and your family.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus told Pilate: my kingdom is not of this world. His kingdom begins in the heart that gives itself to him.

Jesus, King of the Universe, today I recognize you as King of my life. Reign in my heart, which so often wants to rule alone. Reign in my home, in my marriage and in my family. Reign in my work and in my money. Reign in my wounds, where I have not yet let you in. I want no other king to enslave me: not fear, not resentment, not sin. Only you.

Mary, Queen and Mother, lead me to your Son.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"My kingdom is not of this world" (John 18:36). The Solemnity of Our Lord Jesus Christ, King of the Universe, closes the liturgical year on the last Sunday before Advent. We ask Jesus to reign in our heart, our home, our work and our wounds, and we entrust ourselves to Mary, Queen and Mother.

What the Church teaches

Pope Pius XI instituted the feast of Christ the King in 1925, in the encyclical Quas Primas, to remind a world tempted by violent ideologies that Jesus is the true King. Today it closes the liturgical year. His kingdom is not built by force: it grows in hearts that freely give themselves to him (Catechism 2105, 2818).
