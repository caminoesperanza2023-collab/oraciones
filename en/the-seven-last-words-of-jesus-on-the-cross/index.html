The 7 Last Words of Jesus on the Cross — Each One Is a Message for You

Jesus spoke seven times from the cross. Each word was chosen. Each word was a message — not just for those standing there 2000 years ago, but for you, right now.

Discover the Seven Last Words of Jesus: forgiveness for his executioners, paradise for the thief, care for his mother, his cry of abandonment, his thirst, his completion, and his surrender. A theology of love in seven sentences.

What the video says

Jesus spoke seven times from the cross. Each word was chosen. Each word was a message not just for those standing there for you right now. The seven last words of Jesus are the final utterances of the son of God before his death [music] gathered from all four gospels.

First, father forgive them for they do not know what they [music] are doing. He prays for his executioners. While they drive nails through his flesh, he asks mercy for them. Second, truly I tell you, today you will be with me [music] in paradise to the good thief, the last saint to be canonized [music] in the Bible with no time for sacraments or good works.

Just a confession of faith. Third, woman, behold your son. Son, behold your mother. He entrusts Mary to John. In his agony, he tends to his family. Fourth, my God, my God, why have you forsaken me? The most mysterious word. Jesus prays Psalm 22, the psalm of the suffering righteous man who is ultimately vindicated by God.

He is not desparing. He is praying scripture. Fifth, I thirst. The most human of all, the God of the universe. thirsty, thirsting also for souls, for love, for us. Sixth, it is finished. Tetalisti, the Greek word means it is completed. It is accomplished. The debt is paid.

Seventh, father, into your hands, I commend my spirit. Not abandonment, but surrender. His last breath is an act of trust. Seven words, a theology of love. Which of these seven words speaks to what you are living right now? Let it find you today.
