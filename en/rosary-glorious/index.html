Holy Rosary Today: Glorious Mysteries Full Rosary Prayed Aloud (Wednesday & Sunday), Pray With Me

HOLY ROSARY TODAY, GLORIOUS MYSTERIES: pray the complete Rosary with me right now, bead by bead, with a voice beside you on every Hail Mary. For Wednesdays and Sundays, and for any day you need hope: Jesus is alive, and the Virgin Mary walks with you.

Before each decade we pause for a short meditation: the Resurrection, the Ascension, the Descent of the Holy Spirit, the Assumption and the Coronation of Mary. You don't need to know the prayers by heart: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, or simply count on your fingers.

Full text of the prayer

Pray the Holy Rosary with me

Pray the Holy Rosary with me right now, with the Glorious Mysteries, the mysteries for Wednesday and Sunday. If you are tired, worried, or out of words, that is all right: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, and if you don't have one, your fingers are enough. Think of one person or one intention, and place it in the hands of the Virgin Mary. Let us begin.

Sign of the Cross and Apostles' Creed

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth; and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord; who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; on the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there he will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy Catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

First Mystery: The Resurrection

The first glorious mystery: the Resurrection of the Lord. It is Sunday, very early. The women come to the tomb and find it empty. Jesus is alive, and death does not have the last word. Think of what feels dead inside you today: a hope, a relationship, the strength to keep going. Bring it here. The one who conquered death can enter that darkness too, and he tells you: do not be afraid. Perhaps God feels far away today, or you feel alone at home, at work, in a hospital. He has not gone away: he waits for you in heaven and walks with you here. Lift your eyes and ask him for hope. Third Mystery: The Descent of the Holy Spirit

The third glorious mystery: the descent of the Holy Spirit. The apostles are praying together with Mary. Suddenly a strong wind comes, tongues as of fire rest on each of them, and fear turns into courage. There are things you cannot do alone: forgive, start over, speak to the one who hurt you. Ask the Holy Spirit now for the strength you lack, and pray for your family, that there may be peace in your home. The Mother who stood at the foot of the cross is now beside her Son, and she does not forget you. Think of your loved ones who have gone before you, and of your own journey. Fifth Mystery: The Coronation of Mary

The fifth glorious mystery: the coronation of Mary as Queen of heaven and earth. The humble servant of Nazareth is crowned by her Son, and she is still a Mother: a queen who takes care of us. Place in her hands your family and your needs today. Call on her the way you call on a mother: my Mother, pray for me. And offer this decade for whoever needs her most. Hail Holy Queen and closing

Hail, holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile, show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen. Let us pray. O God, whose only begotten Son, by his life, death and resurrection, has purchased for us the rewards of eternal life, grant, we beseech thee, that while meditating on these mysteries of the most holy Rosary of the Blessed Virgin Mary, we may imitate what they contain and obtain what they promise, through the same Christ our Lord. Amen. Thank you for praying with me. May the Virgin Mary watch over you and everyone you love. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
