Carlo Acutis: The Teen Gamer Who Became a Saint

Carlo was an ordinary Italian teenager in almost every way. He loved his dog, played soccer, and taught himself computer programming. But he used his tech skills for something no one expected: he built a website cataloguing Eucharistic miracles from around the world, determined to prove to skeptics that the Eucharist is real.

In 2024, the Catholic Church officially declared him a saint — the first millennial saint, and one of the youngest non-martyrs ever canonized. His story proves that holiness isn't old-fashioned or reserved for monks and nuns centuries ago. You can love technology, games, and friends, and still become a saint.

Carlo's own words: 'Not me, but God.' If a teenager could become a saint in the age of smartphones, so can you.
