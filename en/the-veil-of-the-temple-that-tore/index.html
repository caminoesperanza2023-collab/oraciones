The Temple Veil Tore From the top Down — Why It Matters

When Jesus died, the Temple veil tore. And Matthew 27:51 records a detail that is almost never explained: it tore from the top down.

The direction is not decoration. That veil sealed off the Holy of Holies, and behind it only the high priest entered, once a year, carrying blood. It was the physical border between God and everyone else.

Had it torn from the bottom up, that would be the work of human hands. From the top down, no one can reach: it is a gesture that can only come from the other side.
