Prayer to Overcome Addiction: Lord, Break These Chains

Prayer to overcome addiction: pray this short Catholic prayer with me for yourself or for someone you love who is trapped in alcohol, drugs or any other addiction.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said: if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.

Lord Jesus, you know this struggle. You know the shame, the broken promises and the falls. We cannot do this alone, so we come to you. Break these chains, one day at a time. Give strength in the moment of temptation. Send good people, good help and a way out. And when we fall, lift us up again, without shame.

Saint Monica, you never stopped praying for your son: pray with us.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Jesus said: "If the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed" (John 8:36). We ask for strength one day at a time, for good help and for mercy after every fall, and we pray with Saint Monica. If you are struggling, please also reach out to a doctor, a support group or a priest: prayer does not replace professional help.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism teaches that the virtue of temperance helps us avoid every kind of excess in food, alcohol, tobacco and medicine (Catechism 2290). Addiction is also an illness, so prayer does not replace doctors, treatment or support groups: grace and help work together. The Venerable Matt Talbot of Dublin, an alcoholic who took the pledge in 1884, stayed sober for over forty years through daily Mass, prayer and the sacraments, and many people ask his intercession today.
