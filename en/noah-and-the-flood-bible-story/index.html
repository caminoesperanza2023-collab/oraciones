Noah and the flood: The Bible Story of Faith, Judgment and God's Promise

God looked at a world full of violence, but one man walked with Him: Noah. Discover the powerful Bible story of Noah's Ark, the great flood, forty days and nights of rain, and the rainbow God placed in the sky as a sign of His everlasting covenant.

That rainbow you see after a storm? It's God keeping His promise. He never forgets. He never fails.

Watch the video

What the video says

God looked at the world and His heart broke. The earth was full of violence and corruption. But there was one man who walked with God. His name was Noah. And God said to him: Build an ark. I am going to send a flood, but I will save you and your family.

Noah obeyed. For decades he built that enormous ark while people mocked him. Then the rain came. Forty days and forty nights of rain. The waters rose above the highest mountains. Every creature outside the ark perished. But inside, God kept His promise. When the waters receded, Noah stepped out and built an altar to God.

And God placed a rainbow in the sky as a sign of His covenant: Never again will I destroy the earth with water. That rainbow you see after a storm? It is God keeping His promise. He never forgets. He never fails.

What the Church teaches

🙏 The story of Noah and the Flood is more than an ancient account; it is a message of faith and hope. While the world was full of corruption, one man walked with God and obeyed Him even when it made no sense. God saved Noah, his family, and the animals, and then placed a rainbow in the sky as the sign of His everlasting covenant. That rainbow you see after a storm is God keeping His promise: He never forgets, He never fails. Noah teaches us to trust and obey God even when others mock us, and to believe that He is faithful through every storm. Do you trust God's promises?
