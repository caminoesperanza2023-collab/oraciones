What the video says

Is purgatory in the Bible? Most people say no. The answer surprises them. In 2 Maccabees, Judas Maccabeus offers prayers and sacrifice for soldiers who died in sin — so they might be forgiven. Why pray for the dead if the dead cannot be helped? Saint Paul in 1 Corinthians 3 describes a purification by fire: "He will be saved, but only as through fire." Jesus Himself spoke of sins forgiven "in the age to come" — implying some sins are addressed after death.

Purgatory is not a second hell. It is God's mercy for those who die in His grace but not yet perfectly clean. It is the final embrace of a perfect God for an imperfect soul before eternal glory. Pray for the souls in purgatory today. They are praying for you.

What the Church teaches

Purgatory is one of the most misunderstood — and most consoling — teachings of the Catholic Church. It is not a 'second chance' nor a lesser hell: it is the final purification of those who die in God's grace but still carry attachments to sin (CCC 1030-1032). Scripture points to it in 2 Maccabees 12:46 ('it is a holy and wholesome thought to pray for the dead') and 1 Corinthians 3:15 ('saved, but only as through fire'). Every soul in purgatory is guaranteed Heaven — that's why the saints called them 'holy souls'. And here's the beautiful part: your prayers actually help them.
