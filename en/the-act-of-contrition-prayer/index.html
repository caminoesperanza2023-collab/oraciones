The Act of Contrition: The Prayer That Opens the Door to God's Mercy

This ancient prayer has brought millions back to God after their darkest moments. Learn what it means and why it's the most powerful prayer of forgiveness.

Watch the video

Text of the prayer

There is a [music] prayer so powerful that Catholics have used it for centuries at the moment of their greatest regret. It opens every confession. The act of contrition is not just words. It is a complete surrender of the heart to God and understanding why it works will change the way you pray forever.

Oh my God, I am heartily sorry for having offended thee. [music] These words have been spoken by saints and sinners, kings and prisoners, the dying and the desperate. The act of contrition is not about feeling sorry enough. It is about turning your will back to God.

The word contrition comes from the Latin contritus, [music] which means crushed, not destroyed. your broken heart in God's hands becomes something new. The prayer acknowledges three things. That sin hurts God. That it hurts you. And that with God's grace, you can do better. That is not weakness.

That is the most courageous act a human being can make. Choosing to stand back up and return to the father. When you pray this prayer and mean it, something happens that no human can undo. God's forgiveness is not earned. It is received and it is complete.

Every time, wherever you are, whatever you have done, God is waiting for that prayer.
