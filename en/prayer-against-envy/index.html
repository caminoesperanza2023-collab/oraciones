Prayer Against Envy: Lord, Protect Me and Free My Heart

Prayer against envy: if envy is eating you up inside or you feel the envy of others, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask God for a free and peaceful heart.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Saint Paul says it: love is not envious. And where God's love lives, envy has no room.

Lord, I confess that sometimes I compare myself and the good of others makes me bitter. Heal that in me. Teach me to give thanks for what I have and to be glad for what my brothers and sisters have. And if someone wishes me harm, cover me with your protection, take away my fear, and bless that person too. I don't want to live comparing myself. I want to live in your peace.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Saint Paul writes: "Love is patient, love is kind; love is not envious" (1 Corinthians 13:4). Against envy we don't need charms or rituals: God's protection and a grateful heart are enough. We also pray for those who envy us, and we entrust our heart to the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism calls envy sadness at the sight of another's good and counts it among the capital sins (Catechism 2539). Its remedy is not a charm but goodwill, humility and gratitude: rejoicing in the good of others (Catechism 2540). And if you fear someone's envy, the Church does not recommend amulets (Catechism 2117): trust in God's protection, pray for that person and stay close to the sacraments.
