Prayer for My Brothers and Sisters: Lord, Keep Us United

Prayer for my brothers and sisters: close to you or far away, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your siblings in God's hands today.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says, how good and pleasant it is when brothers live together in unity.

Lord, I place my brothers and sisters in your hands. Bless their homes, their health and their work. Heal the old wounds between us, the words we should not have said, and the years of silence. Where there is distance, bring us back together. Keep us united until the end of our lives.

Virgin Mary, mother of our family, gather your children under your mantle.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"How good and pleasant it is when brothers live together in unity!" (Psalm 133:1). We ask God to bless our siblings, heal old wounds and keep our family united, under the mantle of the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

Psalm 133 sings: "How good and pleasant it is when brothers live together in unity." The family is the first place where we learn to love and to forgive, and the Church calls it a domestic church (Catechism 2204-2206). When there are wounds between siblings, Jesus asks us to seek reconciliation before bringing our gift to the altar (Matthew 5:23-24). Prayer prepares the heart for that first step.
