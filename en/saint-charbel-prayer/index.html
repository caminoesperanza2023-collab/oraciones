Saint Charbel Prayer for Healing: Pray With Me

Saint charbel prayer for healing: if you or someone you love needs healing, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask the intercession of the holy monk of Lebanon.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: the prayer of a righteous person has great power.

Saint Charbel, humble monk of Lebanon, you spent your life in silence, prayer and penance, and after your death God has worked many wonders through your intercession. Today I come to you with my need. Ask Jesus for the healing of my body and of my soul, and for the health of those I love. And if God's answer is not what I expect, give me peace to accept his will.

Saint Charbel, pray for us.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"The prayer of a righteous person has great power in its effects" (James 5:16). Saint Charbel Makhlouf (1828-1898) was a Maronite monk and hermit in Lebanon, canonized by Saint Paul VI in 1977; many healings have been attributed to his intercession. We ask him to pray for us, trusting that God heals as he wills.

What the Church teaches

Saints do not heal by their own power: they intercede for us before God, like friends who pray for us (Catechism 956). Saint Charbel lived 23 years as a hermit in prayer and silence. After his death in 1898 his body remained incorrupt for decades, and his tomb at Annaya is visited by Christians and Muslims alike.
