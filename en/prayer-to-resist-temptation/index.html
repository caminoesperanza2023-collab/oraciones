Prayer to Resist Temptation: Lord, Make Me Stronger Than This

Prayer to resist temptation: if you keep falling into the same sin and you are tired of starting over, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask God for the strength to be free.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: God will not let you be tempted beyond your strength. He always gives you a way out.

Lord, you know my weakness better than anyone. You know the moments when I am alone, tired or sad, and the temptation feels stronger than me. In that moment, remind me who I am: your beloved child. Give me the strength to say no and the humility to ask for help. Take away what pulls me down, and fill that emptiness with you. And when I fall, don't let me hide from you. Lift me up again.

Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond your strength, but with the temptation he will also provide the way out" (1 Corinthians 10:13). We bring our weakness to Jesus, who was also tempted in the desert, and ask Saint Michael the Archangel to defend us in battle.

What the Church teaches

Being tempted is not a sin: Jesus himself was tempted in the desert. When we say "lead us not into temptation" we ask the Father not to let us give in, and that request is a decision of the heart, sustained by prayer and vigilance (Catechism 2846-2849). And if we fall, confession is where God lifts us up again.
