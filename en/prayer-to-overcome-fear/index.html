Prayer to Overcome Fear: Jesus, Take Hold of My Hand

Prayer to overcome fear: if fear won't let you rest, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place what frightens you in the hands of Jesus.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

In the middle of the storm Jesus said: take heart, it is I, do not be afraid.

Lord, I am afraid. I am afraid of what I don't know, of what may happen, of not being able to go on. You see this weight on my chest. Come into this storm with me. Calm my mind and slow my heart. Take from me the thoughts that frighten me, and give me just enough courage for today. I don't ask to see the whole road. I only ask you not to let go of my hand.

Virgin Mary, cover me with your mantle and stay with me tonight.

Amen.

What the Bible says

When the disciples were terrified in the storm, Jesus said to them: "Take heart, it is I; do not be afraid" (Matthew 14:27). This prayer asks for courage for today, not for a life without problems, and we entrust it to the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

Feeling fear is not a sin: Jesus himself felt anguish in Gethsemane (Luke 22:44). Saint Paul teaches us to bring our worries to God in prayer so that his peace may guard our hearts (Philippians 4:6-7). Prayer does not replace other help: if fear stops you from living, talking to a doctor, a counselor or a priest is also a way of taking care of the life God gave you.
