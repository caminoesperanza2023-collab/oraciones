Prayer for Loneliness: Lord, I Feel So Alone, Stay With Me

Prayer for loneliness: if you feel alone, even surrounded by people, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let God keep you company.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

God says it through the prophet Isaiah: do not fear, for I am with you. You are not alone.

Lord, the house is quiet and my heart is heavy. Some days nobody calls and nobody asks how I am. You know this loneliness. In the garden, your friends fell asleep and left you alone. Stay with me tonight. Fill this silence with your presence. Send me a friend, a call, a kind word. And open my eyes to others who are alone too, so I can keep them company.

Virgin Mary, mother who never leaves, sit with me today.

Amen.

What the Bible says

God says through Isaiah: "Do not fear, for I am with you" (Isaiah 41:10). Jesus himself knew loneliness in the garden of Gethsemane. Praying does not replace reaching out to people: it gives you the strength to do it. If loneliness weighs on you very heavily, talk to someone you trust or to a professional. We entrust you to the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

Jesus himself knew loneliness: in Gethsemane he asked his friends to stay awake with him, and they fell asleep (Matthew 26:38-40). That is why he understands yours. Prayer is a real encounter with God (Catechism 2560), but it does not replace the people around you: look for your parish, a group, a neighbor. And if loneliness turns into hopelessness, ask for help today.
