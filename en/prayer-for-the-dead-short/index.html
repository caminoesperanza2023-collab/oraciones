Prayer for a Loved One Who Has Died: Eternal Rest Grant unto Them

Prayer for the dead: if you are missing a loved one who has gone home to God, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and entrust them to the mercy of Jesus. For All Souls' Day (November 2) and every time your heart misses them.

Pray along with the video

Full text

Lord Jesus, you said: I am the resurrection and the life.

Today I bring before you the one I loved so much. Thank you for every day you lent them to me, for their laughter, their hands, everything they taught me. Forgive their faults, purify their soul with your mercy, and welcome them into your house, where there are no more tears and no more pain.

And give me comfort and hope, until the day we meet again in you. Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them. May they rest in peace.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"I am the resurrection and the life" (John 11:25). "Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them."

What the Church teaches

The Church has always prayed for the dead: already in 2 Maccabees 12:46 a sacrifice is offered for the dead so that they may be freed from sin. On November 2, All Souls' Day, we entrust every soul to God's mercy. "Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord" is one of the oldest prayers of the Catholic tradition, and our hope comes from Jesus: "I am the resurrection and the life" (John 11:25).
