The Miracle of Fatima — What 70000 People Actually Witnessed in 1917

On October 13, 1917, 70,000 people — atheists and journalists included — witnessed something science has never been able to explain.

The sun spun for 12 minutes. Changed colors. Then appeared to plunge toward earth. And then — the ground was completely dry, instantly.

God performed this miracle so that you would know: He is real. He sees you. And He is still working.

What the video says

On October 13, 1917, 70,000 people gathered in a muddy field in Portugal. Atheists, journalists, and skeptics included, all sent by newspapers hoping to expose a hoax. What they saw next has never been scientifically [music] explained. For six months, three young shepherd children, Lucia, Francisco, and Justinta, had been seeing the Virgin Mary in that same field.

She had promised, "On October 13th, I will perform a miracle so that everyone will believe." It had been raining all morning, and the crowd was soaked, [music] cold, and doubtful. Then at noon, the clouds suddenly parted and the sun itself began to spin. It changed colors, red, yellow, blue, green, spinning for almost 12 [music] minutes before appearing to plunge straight toward the earth.

People screamed and [music] fell to their knees, certain the world was ending. Then, in an instant, everything went still. And here is what no one could ever explain. The ground was completely dry. Their soaked clothes were completely dry. The Vatican investigated for 13 years, interviewing witnesses and scientists before officially declaring it a true miracle.

God didn't perform that miracle for 1917 [music] alone. He did it so that you right now would know he is real and still working. Did you know about the miracle [music] of Fatima?

What the Church teaches

On October 13, 1917, at Fátima, Portugal, about 70,000 people — believers and atheists, journalists and scientists — watched the sun spin, change colors and plunge toward the earth. Secular newspapers like O Século reported it. It remains the only public miracle in history announced in advance: three shepherd children said Our Lady promised a sign 'so that all may believe.' God did not perform that miracle for 1917 alone. He did it so that you, today, would know He is real and still working.
