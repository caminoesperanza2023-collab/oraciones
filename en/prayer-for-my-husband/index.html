Prayer for My Husband: Lord, Bless and Protect Him

Prayer for my husband: pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your husband in God's hands today, for his work, his health, your marriage, or his eternal rest.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Saint Paul wrote that love is patient and love is kind, and that it never fails.

Lord, today I place my husband in your hands. Bless his work and his health. Give him strength when he is tired and peace when he worries. Heal what has been wounded between us, teach us to forgive quickly, and keep our love faithful until the end. If he has already gone home, grant him eternal rest.

Saint Joseph, faithful husband of Mary, guide him, protect our home, and stay with us in every trial.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Love is patient, love is kind... Love never fails" (1 Corinthians 13:4-8). We ask Saint Joseph, the faithful husband of Mary, to guide and protect him.

What the Church teaches

Marriage is a sacrament: Christ himself strengthens the spouses to love each other faithfully and to forgive (Catechism 1641-1642). Saint Paul describes that love in 1 Corinthians 13: patient, kind, never failing. Saint Joseph, the just and faithful husband of Mary (Matthew 1:19-24), is the patron of husbands and of families.
