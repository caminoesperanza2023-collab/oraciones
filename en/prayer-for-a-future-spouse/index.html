Prayer for a Future Spouse: Lord, Lead Me to the Right Person

Prayer for a future spouse: if you long to meet the person God has for you and you are tired of waiting, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and trust God with your future love.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: it is not good for man to be alone. God himself thought of love before you did.

Lord, you know the desire of my heart. I don't want just anyone, I want the person you have for me. Wherever they are right now, protect them, guide them, and bring us together in your time. Heal my wounds from past relationships, so I can love freely. Give me the patience to wait, the wisdom to recognize them, and the courage to be the person they need. Let our love have you at the center.

Saint Joseph, faithful husband of Mary, pray for me.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"It is not good that the man should be alone" (Genesis 2:18). God created love and knows your heart better than you do. We ask him to prepare us and the person he has for us, and we entrust this desire to Saint Joseph, faithful husband of the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

In the Church, marriage is a sacrament: a path to holiness for two people, not only a feeling (Catechism 1601). Waiting is not wasted time: it is the time to grow, to heal and to become the person you will one day give yourself to.
