50 Years Without Feeling God: What Mother Teresa Revealed

You pray and feel nothing. No comfort, no peace, no answer. Just silence.

Before you decide God left or that you did something wrong, there's a fact you should know.

When Mother Teresa of Calcutta died and her private letters were published, the world found something almost nobody expected: for nearly fifty years she lived without feeling God's presence. She wrote it in her own words, speaking of emptiness and darkness. And in those same fifty years she built an entire congregation and picked the dying up off the streets, one by one.

You pray and feel nothing. No comfort, no peace, no answer, just silence. Before you decide God left or that you did something wrong, there's a fact you should know. When Mother Teresa of Kolkata died and her private letters were published, [music] the world found something almost nobody expected.

For nearly 50 years, she lived without feeling God's presence. She wrote it in her own words, speaking of emptiness and darkness. And in those same 50 years, she built an entire congregation and picked the dying up off the streets one by one. She didn't stop believing when she stopped feeling.

And that is exactly what the tradition calls the dark night. Not a punishment, not a sign you got something wrong, but a faith learning to stand without an immediate reward. Continuing when you feel nothing isn't weak faith. It's the strongest kind there is. If you're in that silence today, you're not alone and you're not doing it wrong.
