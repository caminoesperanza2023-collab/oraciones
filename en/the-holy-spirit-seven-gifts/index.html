The 7 Gifts of the Holy Spirit: Which One Do You Need Today?

The Holy Spirit is not an abstract force. He is a Divine Person who has lived in you since your Baptism. He came with 7 extraordinary gifts: Wisdom, Understanding, Counsel, Fortitude, Knowledge, Piety, and Fear of the Lord.

These gifts are already in you — they just need to be awakened by prayer.

What the video says

The Holy Spirit is not an abstract force. He is a divine person who has lived inside you since the day of your baptism. And he came with seven extraordinary gifts. Wisdom to see life through God's eyes. Understanding to grasp the truths of faith at a deeper level.

Counsel to make right decisions in difficult moments. Fortitude to persevere when everything gets hard. Knowledge to understand created things in their proper relationship to God. Piety to relate to God as father, not as judge. And fear of the Lord, not fear of punishment, but deep reverence before the holiness of the one who loves you infinitely.

These gifts are already in you, but they need to be awakened by prayer. Tonight, ask the Holy Spirit to stir in you the gift you need most right
