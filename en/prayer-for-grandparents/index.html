Prayer for Grandparents: Lord, Take Care of Those Who Took Care of Us

Prayer for grandparents: pray this short Catholic prayer with me for your grandmother and grandfather, here on earth or already with God, and ask him to care for them.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

God said: even to your old age I am he, and to gray hairs I will carry you.

Lord, thank you for the hands that held our family together. Thank you for their prayers, for their stories and for everything they gave up without saying a word. Take care of them when their strength runs out. Stay with them on the long and lonely days. Heal their pain, calm their fears, and never let them feel forgotten. And to those who are already with you, give them eternal rest.

Saint Joachim and Saint Anne, grandparents of Jesus, pray for them. Virgin Mary, keep them close.

Amen.

What the Bible says

God promises: "Even to your old age I am he, and to gray hairs I will carry you" (Isaiah 46:4). We pray for the grandparents who held our families together, and we entrust them to Saint Joachim and Saint Anne, the grandparents of Jesus, and to the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

Tradition gives the names Joachim and Anne to the parents of the Virgin Mary, the grandparents of Jesus, and the Church celebrates them on July 26. Pope Francis instituted the World Day for Grandparents and the Elderly in 2021, held on the fourth Sunday of July. Honoring and caring for the old belongs to the Fourth Commandment (Catechism 2218), and Scripture calls us to treasure them: "Do not cast me off in the time of old age" (Psalm 71:9).
