Prayer for a New Beginning: Lord, Help Me Start Again

Prayer for a new beginning: if you feel your life fell apart and you don't know how to start again, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let God write a new chapter with you.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: behold, I am doing a new thing. Now it springs up; do you not see it?

Lord, I give you everything that ended: the plans that broke, the doors that closed, the people who left. I don't want to live looking back. Heal what still hurts and forgive what I did wrong. Give me the courage to take the first step today, even if it is small. Open the doors that only you can open, and close the ones that would hurt me.

Virgin Mary, Mother of hope, walk with me.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Behold, I am doing a new thing; now it springs forth, do you not perceive it?" (Isaiah 43:19). God does not erase our story: he redeems it. We entrust to him what ended, ask him to heal what still hurts, and walk forward with the Virgin Mary, Mother of hope.

What the Church teaches

In the parable of the prodigal son, the father does not wait for explanations: he runs to embrace his son and throws a feast (Luke 15:20-24). That is how God receives every new beginning. Confession is the most concrete way to start again with him (Catechism 1439).
