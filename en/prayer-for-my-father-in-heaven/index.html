Prayer for My Father in Heaven: Lord, Welcome Him Home

Prayer for my father in heaven: if your dad has passed away and you miss him, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask God to give him eternal rest in the Father's house.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus promised it: in my Father's house are many rooms; I go to prepare a place for you.

Lord, today I entrust my father to you. Thank you for his work, his sacrifices and the strength he gave our family. Forgive what he did wrong, and remember every good thing he did, even the things no one saw. Eternal rest grant unto him, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon him. And help me to honor him with my life, until we meet again in your house.

Saint Joseph, good and faithful father, intercede for him before God.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"In my Father's house are many rooms... I go to prepare a place for you" (John 14:2). Losing a father leaves an empty chair that no one else fills. But our love can still reach him: the Church teaches that our prayers, our good works and above all the Mass can help those who have died.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism teaches that those who die in God's grace but are still imperfectly purified undergo purification after death, and that from the beginning the Church has offered prayers for them, above all the Eucharistic sacrifice, as well as almsgiving, indulgences and works of penance (Catechism 1030-1032). Having a Mass offered for your father is a real help to him.
