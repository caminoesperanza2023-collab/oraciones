Psalm 91 — God's Shield of Protection | Pray This Prayer Every Day

"He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High will rest in the shadow of the Almighty." — Psalm 91:1

God promises to protect YOU. Pray this powerful Psalm 91 prayer right now and place your life under His divine shield. His angels guard you in all your ways.

Text of the prayer

If you feel afraid today, this ancient prayer has protected millions. Psalm 91, the shield of the most high. Written 3,000 years ago, this psalm is God's direct promise to you. Every verse is a shield. Stay with me. Say these words and receive that protection.

Pray with me. Lord, I dwell in your shelter today. You are my refuge and my fortress. My God, in you I trust. You will cover me with your feathers, and under your wings I will find rest. No weapon formed against me shall prosper. Your angels will guard me in all my ways.

I am not afraid of the darkness, for your light surrounds me. I call on your name, Lord, and you answer me. You are with me in trouble. You deliver me and give me long life. Thank you, Father. Amen. Right now, as you hear this, God's angels are around you.

You are already covered. His promise never fails. Psalm 91 is not just poetry. It is your reality. Pray Psalm 91 every morning. Did this touch your heart?
