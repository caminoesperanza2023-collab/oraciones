A spring appeared where Mary asked Bernadette to dig. Over 7,000 cures have been claimed at Lourdes. 70 have been declared officially miraculous by the medical bureau — cases where science had no explanation.

Bernadette entered a convent and asked to be forgotten. Her body, exhumed 30 years after death, was found incorrupt.
