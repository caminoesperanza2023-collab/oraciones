St. Therese's Little Way How a 24-Year-Old Nun Became a Doctor of the Church

She died at 24 having done nothing the world calls great — and today she is a Doctor of the Church. Discover the Little Way of Saint Therese of Lisieux.

You don't need to do great things to become a saint. You need to love greatly in the small things.

What the video says

She died at 24 having done nothing the world calls great and today she is a doctor of the church. What was her secret? TZ of Lissu entered the Carmelite convent at 15 and lived hidden [music] from the world. No missions, no miracles in her lifetime, no famous works.

Her greatness was a path she called the little way. Doing small things with great love. Smiling at the sister who annoyed her. Offering up a cold meal without complaint. Climbing into his arms instead of trying to climb the stairs of perfection alone. Before dying, she promised, "I will spend my heaven doing good on earth.

And I will let fall a shower of roses. Millions have asked her intercession and received it. You don't need to do great things to become a saint. You need to love greatly in the small things. That is the little way. St. TZ, teach us your little way.
