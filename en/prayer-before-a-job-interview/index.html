Prayer Before a Job Interview: Lord, Calm My Nerves

Prayer before a job interview: if you have an interview today or soon, pray this short Catholic prayer with me for calm, clarity and God's favor.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says: do not be anxious about anything, but present your requests to God.

Lord, I place this interview in your hands. Calm my nerves and clear my mind. Help me remember what I have prepared, speak with honesty and confidence, and show who I really am. Give favor to my words before the people who will listen to me. And if this is not the door for me, open the one you have prepared.

Saint Joseph, patron of workers, go with me.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything, by prayer and petition, present your requests to God" (Philippians 4:6). We ask for calm nerves, honest words and an open door, with Saint Joseph the Worker, patron of all who work.

What the Church teaches

The Church teaches that work is a right and a duty: through it we provide for our families, serve others and share in God's work of creation (Catechism 2427-2428). Saint Joseph, the carpenter of Nazareth, is the patron of workers, and the Church celebrates him as Saint Joseph the Worker every May 1. Saint Paul reminds us not to be anxious but to bring every request to God, and his peace will guard our hearts (Philippians 4:6-7).
