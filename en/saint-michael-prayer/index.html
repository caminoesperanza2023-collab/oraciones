Text of the prayer

When fear feels close, pray this powerful Catholic prayer with me. Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle. Be our protection against the wickedness and snares of the devil. May God rebuke him, we humbly pray. And do thou, O Prince of the heavenly host, by the power of God, cast into hell Satan and all the evil spirits who prowl about the world seeking the ruin of souls.

Amen. Saint Michael, guard my mind, my home, and everyone I love. Help me resist temptation without hatred, panic, or despair. Keep my eyes fixed on Christ, whose victory is already complete. Let courage replace fear and faithful action replace anxiety. Save this prayer, and send it to someone who needs protection today.

What the Church teaches

A short note on the Saint Michael prayer ⚔️ Pope Leo XIII wrote it in 1886 and asked that it be prayed after every Low Mass, which it was for almost eighty years. Many parishes have brought it back. The words are strong on purpose: defend, protection, snares, cast into hell. It is a prayer for battle. But notice who fights. Not you. Not even Michael, on his own strength. Every line points to God: may God rebuke him, by the power of God. The Catholic tradition is clear that the devil is a creature, already defeated at the Cross, and that our part is not to fear him but to stay close to Christ. That is why the second half of this short matters: guard my mind, my home, and everyone I love; help me resist without hatred, panic or despair. The Saint Michael prayer is not about seeing demons everywhere. It is about not being afraid. Pray it in the morning, before sleep, or when fear gets close. Then get on with your day.
