Prayer When Money Is Tight: Lord, Give Us Our Daily Bread

Prayer when money is tight: if the bills are piling up and the money is not enough, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your needs in God's hands.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said, look at the birds of the air. Your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not worth much more than they?

Lord, you know every bill on my table and every worry that keeps me awake. Give us today our daily bread. Open a door of work, give me wisdom to use well what I have, and good people to help us. Free me from fear, and never let our home lose its peace.

Saint Joseph, who worked with your hands to feed the Holy Family, take care of my family too.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Look at the birds of the air... your heavenly Father feeds them" (Matthew 6:26). We ask for our daily bread (Matthew 6:11) and for the help of Saint Joseph, who worked to feed the Holy Family. This is a prayer of trust, not a promise of quick money.

What the Church teaches

Jesus teaches us to ask for our daily bread (Matthew 6:11) and not to be crushed by anxiety, because the Father knows what we need (Matthew 6:26-33). Trust in Providence does not mean doing nothing: Saint Joseph worked with his hands to support the Holy Family. The Church asks us to pray, to work, to manage our goods wisely and to help one another (Catechism 2830-2833). God does not promise riches, but he never abandons those who trust in him.
