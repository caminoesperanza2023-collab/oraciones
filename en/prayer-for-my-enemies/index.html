Prayer for My Enemies: Lord, Help Me Bless Those Who Hurt Me

Prayer for my enemies: if someone is against you, speaks badly of you or wants to see you fall, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let God protect your heart from hatred.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said it: love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you.

Lord, you know who they are and what they have done. I do not want to carry hatred in my heart. Protect me from their words and their plans. But also touch their hearts, Lord. Bless them, even if it costs me to say it. Free me from the desire for revenge, and let your justice, not mine, have the last word.

Saint Stephen, who forgave those who stoned you, pray for me.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you" (Matthew 5:44). Praying for those who hurt us does not mean approving what they do: it means not letting their evil take over our hearts. We ask God for protection and peace, and we entrust ourselves to Saint Stephen, who forgave those who stoned him (Acts 7:60).

What the Church teaches

Loving our enemies is one of the most demanding teachings of Jesus, and the Church calls it the summit of Christian prayer and love (Catechism 2844). It does not mean pretending nothing happened or staying in danger: it means asking God for their good and refusing to let hatred decide for us.
