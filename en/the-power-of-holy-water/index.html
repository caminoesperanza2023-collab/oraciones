What the video says

It sits at the entrance of your church and you may have never known everything it can do. Holy water is not an empty symbol. Holy water is a sacramental blessed by a priest. It recalls your baptism and disposes your soul to receive God's grace.

When you sign yourself with it, you renew your baptismal promises and ask protection against evil. St. She kept it beside her always. The church has used it since the earliest centuries. Blessing homes, the sick, the fields, the faithful. You can keep it at home.

Bless your children before they sleep. Bless your doorways. Bless yourself in moments of fear or temptation. Its power is not in the water itself. It is in the prayer of the church and in [music] your faith in Christ who conquered evil. Next time you pass the font, pause and bless yourself slowly.
