What the video says

You wake up at 3:00 in the morning again and the internet will tell you it's the devil's hour. The church teaches that nowhere. It's modern folklore. What does exist is far older and far better. For more than 1,500 years, monasteries have risen in the dead of night to pray.

The rule of St. Benedict calls it vigils. The hour when the world goes quiet and nothing competes for you. They didn't choose it out of fear. They chose it because at that hour nobody is fighting for your attention. And Psalm 119 said it even earlier at midnight I rise to give you thanks.

So next time your eyes open at that hour, you don't have to be afraid or hunt for a hidden meaning. You can do what monks have done for centuries. Say one short line and go back to sleep. Turn the insomnia into a vigil. Does this happen to you, too?
