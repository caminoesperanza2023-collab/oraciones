Jesus Let His Friend Die On Purpose — Then Did the Unthinkable | Lazarus

Jesus received word that His friend Lazarus was dying — and He deliberately waited two more days. By the time He arrived, Lazarus had been four days in the tomb.

But Jesus wasn't late. He was setting the stage for the most stunning miracle of His earthly ministry. When Martha ran to meet Him, He spoke the words that still shake the world: 'I am the resurrection and the life.' Then He stood before the tomb and commanded: 'Lazarus, come forth!' — and the dead man walked out, still wrapped in his burial cloths.

This miracle from John 11 proves that no tomb is final, no situation is too far gone, and no goodbye is the end of the story. Whatever feels dead in your life today — a marriage, a dream, your hope — He can still say 'come forth.'

What the Church teaches

The raising of Lazarus in John 11 is more than a miracle — it is a promise. Jesus deliberately waited, not because He didn't care, but because He was about to reveal something greater: 'I am the resurrection and the life.' Notice that before He called Lazarus out of the tomb, Jesus wept. The Son of God stood at the grave of His friend and cried — He does not stay distant from your pain, He steps into it. And then He spoke four words that death could not resist: 'Lazarus, come forth.' Whatever seems dead in your life today — a relationship, a dream, your hope, your faith — bring it to Him.
