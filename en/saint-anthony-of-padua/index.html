Saint Anthony of Padua: The Miracle Worker Catholics Love

What the video says

St. Anthony of Padua was born in Lisbon in 1195, but is called of Padua because that's where he died and where his relics rest. He lived only 36 years and in that time he changed the world. He was the most powerful preacher of his era.

It's said that when he preached in coastal cities, the fish in the sea would gather to listen. Once when heretics refused to hear him, the fish put their heads above the water. His most famous miracle involves lost things. A novice stole his treasured book of Psalms.

Anthony prayed. The novice was terrified by visions and returned the book. Today, people ask him for lost objects, but also for lost people. loved ones lost in addiction, in broken relationships, in lost faith. St. Anthony of Padua, pray for us. What the Church teaches

This video is for you if you needed a reminder today that you are not alone. Faith is not believing everything will be perfect. It's believing God is present in everything — in what goes well and in what hurts. That changes everything. Saint Paul said it better than anyone ever could: "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." Not all the things I want. All things — including forgiving, waiting, getting back up, and moving forward. If this video found you today, it wasn't by accident. God speaks through the ordinary.
