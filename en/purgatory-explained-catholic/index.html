Purgatory Explained: What Catholics Actually Believe

Purgatory is not a second hell. It is not a second chance at salvation. It's the final purification process for souls already saved — before entering fully into God's presence.

1 Corinthians 3: the one whose work doesn't survive will be saved — but only as through fire. This is the mercy of God.

What the video says

Purgatory is one of the most misunderstood topics in Catholicism. It is not a second hell. It is not a second chance at salvation. It is something entirely different. Imagine a friend lends you money and you pay them back. The debt is settled, but the strain it put on your relationship still needs to heal.

That's what purgatory is. In confession, God forgives the sin. But the temporal consequences of that sin, the damage it caused to our soul still need to be purified. Purgatory is that final process of purification. St. Paul speaks of this in first Corinthians 3. The day of the Lord will reveal each person's work.

If the work doesn't survive, the builder will be saved, but only as through fire. The good news, purgatory is a sign of God's mercy. It means God doesn't discard imperfect souls. He purifies them so they can enter his presence. Pray today for the souls in purgatory.
