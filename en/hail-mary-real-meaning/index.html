What the Church teaches

The Hail Mary is not vain repetition — every word is Scripture and history. 'Hail, full of grace' — the words of the angel Gabriel (Luke 1:28). 'Blessed are you among women' — Elizabeth's greeting, filled with the Holy Spirit (Luke 1:42). And the second half is the Church's oldest plea: 'pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death' — the two moments that matter most.
