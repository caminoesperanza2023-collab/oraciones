What the video says

One day, three disciples climbed a mountain with Jesus and saw something that left them speechless. This is the Transfiguration, and it reveals who Jesus truly is. Moses and Elijah appeared beside him, and a voice came from a bright cloud saying: This is my beloved Son.

Listen to him. Jesus showed them his glory to strengthen them before his suffering on the cross. And he shows us too: there is glory beyond every cross. Which part moved you most?

What the Church teaches

On Mount Tabor, Jesus was transfigured: his face shone like the sun and his garments became dazzling white. Peter, James and John saw his hidden glory as God — to strengthen them before the scandal of the cross. The Transfiguration reminds us that beyond every suffering there is glory, that the Christ who suffers is the same Lord of light.
