Text of the prayer

Her husband was murdered. Her sons swore revenge. And this woman prayed for something no one saw coming. They call her the saint of impossible causes. And when you hear what she survived and the wound that appeared on her forehead, [music] you'll understand why millions still turn to her when every door has closed.

Rita lived in 15th century Italy. She dreamed of becoming a nun, but her family forced her to marry a harsh, violent man. For 18 years, she endured him, and slowly her patience softened his heart. Then he was ambushed and killed. Her two sons [music] wanted blood.

Rita faced an impossible choice. Watch her children become murderers or lose them. She begged God to [music] take them before they could sin that way. and both fell ill and died at peace, forgiving their father's killers. [music] Alone, everything lost. Rita finally entered the convent she had longed for since childhood.

But her story wasn't over. A single thorn from Christ's crown pierced her forehead, a wound that stayed and bled for 15 years. She carried it until she died. When her body was exumed, it was found incorrupt. It remains so to this day. So whatever feels impossible in your life today, remember Rita.

Bring it to her. [music] Did her story move you?

What the Church teaches

Saint Rita of Cascia is called the saint of impossible causes for a reason. She endured 18 years of a violent marriage and softened her husband's heart through patience and prayer. When he was murdered and her sons swore revenge, she prayed the unthinkable — that God would take them before they became murderers. She lost everything, entered the convent, and received the wound of Christ's crown of thorns that bled on her forehead for 15 years. Her body remains incorrupt in Cascia, Italy, to this day. Whatever feels impossible in your life right now — a broken family, an illness, a situation with no way out — Saint Rita understands, and she intercedes.
