Marian apparitions: Fatima, Lourdes & Guadalupe — When Heaven Touched Earth

Throughout history the Virgin Mary has appeared with extraordinary messages. Our Lady of Guadalupe left her image on a cloak that science cannot explain. At Lourdes she said: I am the Immaculate Conception. At Fatima, 70,000 people witnessed the Miracle of the Sun.

These apparitions, approved by the Catholic Church after rigorous investigation, all carry one message: pray, repent, and return to God. Our Lady is calling. Will you answer?

What the video says

Throughout history, the Virgin Mary has appeared to ordinary people with extraordinary messages. In 1531, she appeared to a poor indigenous man in Mexico. Juan Diego. She left her image miraculously imprinted on his cloak — a cloak that scientists cannot explain after five centuries. She is Our Lady of Guadalupe.

In 1858, she appeared eighteen times to a fourteen-year-old girl named Bernadette in a cave in Lourdes, France. She said: I am the Immaculate Conception. A spring of water appeared. Millions have been healed. In 1917, in Fatima, Portugal, she appeared to three shepherd children and predicted war, the spread of Russia's errors, and peace through prayer.

On October 13, 1917, seventy thousand people witnessed the sun spin and dance in the sky — the Miracle of the Sun. These apparitions have been approved by the Catholic Church after rigorous investigation. The message is always the same: pray, repent, and return to God.

Our Lady is calling. Will you answer?

What the Church teaches

🙏 Throughout history the Virgin Mary has appeared to ordinary people with extraordinary messages: Our Lady of Guadalupe in Mexico, Lourdes in France, and Fatima in Portugal, where 70,000 people witnessed the Miracle of the Sun. The Catholic Church approved these apparitions after rigorous investigation. In each one, Our Lady brings the same call: pray, repent, and return to God. She never points to herself; she always leads us to her Son Jesus. These apparitions are the tenderness of God taking the face of a Mother who does not want any of her children to be lost.
