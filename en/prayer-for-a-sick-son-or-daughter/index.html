Prayer for a Sick Son or Daughter: Lord, Heal My Child

Prayer for a sick son or daughter: if your son or daughter is ill, in the hospital or going through treatment, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your child in the hands of Jesus.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

A father begged Jesus for his sick son, and Jesus told him: go, your son will live.

Lord Jesus, you know how much I love my child, no matter how old they are. Place your hand where the illness hurts most. Guide the doctors, make the treatments work and give them strength for each day. Take away their fear and do not let them lose hope. And give me the peace to stay by their side without falling apart. My child is yours before they are mine.

Mother of Sorrows, who knew this pain, pray for my child.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Go; your son will live" (John 4:50). A royal official begged Jesus to come before his son died, and Jesus healed him with a single word. No matter how old our children are, their pain is ours. Bring it to Jesus and, if the illness is serious, ask a priest for the Anointing of the Sick.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism teaches that Christ's compassion toward the sick and his many healings are a resplendent sign that God has visited his people (Catechism 1503), and that the Anointing of the Sick is not only for those at the point of death, but for any of the faithful who begin to be in danger of death from sickness or old age (Catechism 1514).
