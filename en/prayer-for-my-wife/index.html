Prayer for My Wife: Lord, Bless and Protect Her

Prayer for my wife: pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your wife in God's hands today, for her health, her peace, your marriage, or her eternal rest.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says, a good wife is a gift from the Lord, worth far more than jewels.

Lord, today I place my wife in your hands. Thank you for her love, her patience and every sacrifice she makes for our home. Give her health, strength and peace. Comfort her when she is tired, heal what has been wounded between us, and teach me to love her as Christ loves his Church.

Virgin Mary, cover her with your mantle and keep our marriage faithful until the end.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"A capable wife... is worth far more than jewels" (Proverbs 31:10), and Saint Paul asks husbands to love their wives as Christ loves the Church (Ephesians 5:25). We place her under the mantle of the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

In the sacrament of Marriage, Christ gives the spouses the grace to love each other with a faithful, fruitful and lasting love (Catechism 1641). Saint Paul asks husbands to love their wives "as Christ loved the Church and gave himself up for her" (Ephesians 5:25), and the Book of Proverbs praises the good wife as worth more than jewels (Proverbs 31:10). Praying for one's spouse every day is one of the simplest ways to protect a marriage.
