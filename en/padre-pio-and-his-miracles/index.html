Padre Pio's Miracles The Saint Who Bore the Wounds of Christ for 50 Years

He carried the wounds of Christ on his body for fifty years — and the miracles attributed to him still defy explanation. Discover the incredible story of Saint Padre Pio.

This humble Capuchin friar received the stigmata in 1918 — doctors examined the wounds for decades and found no natural explanation. Witnesses said he read souls in confession, appeared in two places at once, and turned away bomber planes in World War II. He heard confessions up to 16 hours a day.

His advice for every anxious soul: "Pray, hope, and don't worry."
