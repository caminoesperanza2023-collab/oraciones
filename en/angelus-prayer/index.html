The Angelus Prayer: Pray It With Me at Noon

The angelus prayer: at noon, Catholics around the world stop for a minute to pray the Angelus. Pray it with me now and remember the moment God became man.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

It remembers the moment the angel announced to Mary that God would become man.

The Angel of the Lord declared unto Mary, and she conceived of the Holy Spirit. Behold the handmaid of the Lord, be it done unto me according to your word. And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Let us pray: Lord, pour your grace into our hearts, so that we who know the incarnation of your Son may come through his cross to the glory of his resurrection. Amen.

Holy Mary, teach me to say yes to God every day.

Amen.

What the Bible says

The Angelus recalls the Annunciation: the angel Gabriel announced to Mary that she would be the Mother of God, and she answered "Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it done unto me according to your word" (Luke 1:38), and "the Word became flesh and dwelt among us" (John 1:14). It is traditionally prayed at 6 am, noon and 6 pm, with a Hail Mary after each verse.

What the Church teaches

The Angelus has been prayed for centuries at the sound of church bells, three times a day, to sanctify ordinary work with the memory of the Incarnation (Catechism 2678 recommends the Angelus among Marian devotions). During Easter time it is replaced by the Regina Caeli. The Pope prays it every Sunday at noon in Saint Peter's Square.
