Prayer for Young People: Lord, Guide Them on Every Path

Prayer for young people: if a young person you love is lost, confused or far from God, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place them in the hands of Jesus.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus looked at the rich young man with love, and he looks at every young person the same way today.

Lord, I pray for the young people of this world, and for the ones I love by name. You know their dreams and their fears, their loneliness and their pressure to fit in. Protect them from addictions, from violence and from voices that lie to them. Give them true friends and show them the value of their lives. And if they have walked away from you, go out and find them.

Saint John Paul the Second, friend of young people, pray for them.

Amen.

What the Bible says

In the Gospel, Jesus looked at the rich young man "and loved him" (Mark 10:21). We pray for young people facing loneliness, pressure, addictions and a world full of noise, and we entrust them to Saint John Paul II, who never stopped believing in them.

What the Church teaches

Saint John Paul II started World Youth Day in 1985 because he believed young people are not the future of the Church but its present. He told them: "Do not be afraid. Open wide the doors for Christ."
