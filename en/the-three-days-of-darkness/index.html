What the Church teaches

🙏 The prophecy of the Three Days of Darkness is one of the most discussed private revelations in Catholic circles. Multiple saints and mystics across different centuries and countries described strikingly similar events: a period of complete darkness covering the earth, during which only blessed candles would give light. Among those who spoke of it: Blessed Anna Maria Taigi, Padre Pio, Saint Elizabeth Canori Mora and others. The consistency of detail across independent sources separated by centuries is what makes this prophecy remarkable. It is important to note: the Church has not defined this as dogma or required belief in it. Private revelation — even from canonized saints — is not binding on Catholics the way Scripture and Tradition are. You are free to believe it or not. What the prophecy does invite us to, regardless of whether it comes literally true, is this: to be spiritually prepared, close to God, and rooted in prayer and the sacraments every day.
