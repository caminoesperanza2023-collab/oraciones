Only 3 Angels Are Named in the Bible — Here's Why They Matter

The Bible only names three angels. And each one has a completely different mission. Michael: the warrior. Gabriel: the messenger. Raphael: the healer.

You can invoke them today. Michael for protection. Gabriel to receive God's Word. Raphael when you're sick or starting something new.

What the video says

The Bible names only three angels, Michael, Gabriel, and Raphael. And each one has a completely different mission. In Revelation, he leads the heavenly army against the dragon. He's the protector of the church and the defender of souls at the moment of death. Gabriel whose name means God is my strength is the messenger.

He announced to Zachchariah the birth of John the Baptist. He appeared to Mary to announce the incarnation. He is the angel of great revelations. Raphael whose name means God heals appears in the book of Tobot as a traveling companion. He heals Tobot's blindness, frees Sarah from a demon, and guides young Tobaya on his journey.

He is the angel of healing and travel. You can invoke them today. Michael for your protection. Gabriel to receive God's word. Raphael when you are ill or beginning something
