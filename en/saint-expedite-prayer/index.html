Saint Expedite Prayer for Urgent Causes: Do Not Leave It for Tomorrow

Saint expedite prayer for urgent causes: if you have an urgent need and cannot wait any longer, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask the intercession of Saint Expedite.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: now is the acceptable time; now is the day of salvation.

Saint Expedite, soldier of Christ, tradition tells that when you decided to convert, you did not leave it for tomorrow. Today I come to you with an urgent need. Ask Jesus to help me in this situation, as soon as it is good for my soul. Give me the courage not to postpone what I must do, and the patience to accept God's timing. And if the answer takes longer, help me keep trusting.

Saint Expedite, pray for us.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Now is the acceptable time; behold, now is the day of salvation" (2 Corinthians 6:2). Saint Expedite is honored as a Roman soldier martyred in Melitene, Armenia, in the 4th century; tradition tells that when he decided to convert, a crow cried "cras" (tomorrow) and he answered "hodie" (today). He is invoked for urgent causes and against procrastination.

What the Church teaches

Little is known historically about Saint Expedite, and the Church invites us to honor the saints without superstition: no prayer "forces" God, and no chain of prayers is required (Catechism 2111). His lesson is simple and very current: do not leave for tomorrow the conversion and the good you can do today.
