The Mother Teresa Quote That Changes Everything

Her words still touch hearts around the world. This quote from Mother Teresa will change how you see love.

Watch the video

What the video says

Mother Teresa said something before she died that no one forgets. She spent her life among the poorest of the poor and she found Jesus in every single one of them. She said, "If you judge people, you have no time to love them. Not all of us can do great things, but we can do small things with great love.

At the end of life, we will not be judged by how much we did, but by how much love we put into what we did. Who can you love today with one small act?
