Prayer for My Daughter: Lord, Bless and Protect Her

Prayer for my daughter: little or grown, close to you or far away, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your daughter in God's hands today.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says: the Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make his face shine upon you.

Lord, today I place my daughter in your hands. Thank you for the gift of her life. Protect her body, her mind and her heart. Guide her decisions, keep her away from every danger and every relationship that would hurt her, and give her friends who bring her closer to you. When she is far from me, let her never be far from you.

Virgin Mary, mother of every daughter, keep her under your mantle.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"The Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make his face shine upon you" (Numbers 6:24-25). We ask God to protect our daughters, guide their decisions and keep them close to him, under the mantle of the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

The blessing of Numbers 6:24-26, "The Lord bless you and keep you", is the blessing God himself gave to his people, and parents can pray it over their children at any age. The Church teaches that parents are the first heralds of the faith for their children and pray for them all their lives (Catechism 2223-2226). Even when a daughter lives far away or makes her own choices, a parent's prayer keeps her before God every day.
