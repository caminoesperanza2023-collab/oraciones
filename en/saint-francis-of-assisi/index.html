Saint Francis of Assisi: The Saint Who Gave Up Everything

Francis was born into wealth, had everything — and one day gave it all away. No bag, no sandals, no gold. He went to the people no one wanted to touch.

He founded the Franciscan Order. He wrote the Peace Prayer. He preached to the birds when men wouldn't listen.

Watch the video

What the video says

Francis of Aisi was born in 1181 to a wealthy merchant family in Italy. He grew up with every comfort money could buy. And then one day everything changed. He heard the words of Matthew 10 read in mass. Go without gold, without silver, without bag, without sandals.

And he decided to take those words literally. He gave away everything including his clothes. His father was furious. The bishop of Aizi was confused. But Francis was at peace. He rebuilt a ruined church with his own hands. He preached to the birds when people wouldn't listen.

He kissed lepers. He founded the Franciscan Order, which today has hundreds of thousands of members worldwide. He wrote the prayer of St. Francis. Lord, make me an instrument of your peace, which is still recited daily by millions. Francis teaches us that simplicity is not poverty.

It's freedom. When you stop clinging to things, you start seeing God everywhere. In people, in creation, in the face of those the world ignores.

What the Church teaches

This video is for you if you needed a reminder today that you are not alone. Faith is not believing everything will be perfect. It's believing God is present in everything — in what goes well and in what hurts. That changes everything. Saint Paul said it better than anyone ever could: "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." Not all the things I want. All things — including forgiving, waiting, getting back up, and moving forward. If this video found you today, it wasn't by accident. God speaks through the ordinary.
