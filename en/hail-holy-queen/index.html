Hail Holy Queen: The 1000-Year-Old Prayer to Mary

For almost a thousand years, Catholics have ended their day with this ancient prayer to Mary. Let her carry to her Son whatever you're too tired to carry yourself.

Text of the prayer

For almost a thousand years, Catholics have ended their day with the same ancient prayer to Mary. It's called the Hail Holy Queen. Hail Holy Queen, Mother of Mercy, our life, our sweetness, and our hope. We call her Queen because her son is king of everything.

To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our size, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. It's an honest prayer. It admits life is hard, but it ends with hope. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us.

Pray it tonight. Before you sleep, let Mary carry to her son whatever you're too tired to carry yourself.
