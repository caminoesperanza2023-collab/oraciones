3 Signs God Is Answering Your Prayer Even When You Cannot See It

Sign 1: Peace that makes no sense. Sign 2: Small doors open unexpectedly. Sign 3: The desire to pray more, not less.

God is not ignoring you. He is building something. Keep praying.

Watch the video

Text of the prayer

Stay right there. Because that exact feeling is usually what happens right before the breakthrough. Scripture says God hears every prayer. Not some, every. The delay is never [music] silence. It's preparation. Here are three signs he's already at work. Sign one, peace. That doesn't make sense.

If you feel unexplained peace in the middle of your storm, that is God holding you. He is answering. You just haven't seen it yet. Sign two. Small doors start opening. Not the door you asked for. A different one. A conversation you didn't expect. An opportunity that comes out of nowhere.

God rarely answers the way we plan. He answers better. [music] Sign three, the desire to pray more, not less. When God is moving, he draws you closer. If you find yourself wanting to pray, even when it feels dry, that pull is him responding to you, even in the silence.

The Psalms are full of people crying out to God and feeling nothing. And then in that very same psalm, praising him [music] for what he was doing all along. God is not ignoring you. He is building something. Keep praying. Not because you feel it, but because he is faithful even when you don't.

What the Church teaches

If you've been praying for weeks and it feels like God isn't listening — stay right there. Scripture shows three signs that God is answering: first, the delay itself (Jesus waited four days before raising Lazarus — delay is not denial); second, doors closing (when God shut Paul's path to Asia, it was to open Europe); third, that restlessness in your heart pushing you to keep praying — that persistence is not weakness, it is the Holy Spirit keeping the request alive in you.
