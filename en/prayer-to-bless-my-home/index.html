Prayer to Bless My Home: Lord, Fill This House with Peace

Prayer to bless my home: pray this short Catholic house blessing with me and ask God to fill your home and everyone who lives in it with peace.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said: when you enter a house, first say, peace to this house.

Lord, bless this home. Let there be peace in our words, patience in our hearts and forgiveness before we sleep. Protect this house from every evil, every danger and every division, and let everyone who enters here feel your love.

Virgin Mary and Saint Joseph, make this house a little Nazareth.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Jesus told his disciples: "Whatever house you enter, first say, 'Peace to this house!'" (Luke 10:5). We ask God to protect our home from every evil and division, and we entrust it to the Holy Family of Nazareth, Mary and Joseph.

What the Church teaches

Blessings are sacramentals: sacred signs instituted by the Church that dispose us to receive grace and sanctify different circumstances of life, including our homes (Catechism 1667-1671). Jesus taught his disciples to bring peace to every house they entered (Luke 10:5), and Joshua declared, "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord" (Joshua 24:15). Many families also ask their parish priest to bless the house with holy water, especially when moving in.
