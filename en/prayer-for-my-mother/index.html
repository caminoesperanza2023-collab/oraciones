Prayer for My Mother: Pray With Me for Her Health, Peace and Life

PRAYER FOR MY MOTHER, prayed together: say your mother's name and pray with me for her, slowly and with no hurry, for about ten minutes. To thank God for her, to pray for her health and peace, or to remember her if she has passed.

We pray Psalm 23, The Lord Is My Shepherd, the Our Father, the Hail Mary, the Sub Tuum Praesidium and the Hail Holy Queen, with five intentions: thanksgiving for her life, for her health, for her peace and faith, for what needs healing between us, and for mothers far away or already gone. We end with a blessing for your mother from the Book of Numbers. You do not need to know the prayers by heart: I will lead and you simply pray along in your heart.

Full text of the prayer

Pray with me for your mother

There is one word we learned before any other, and we still say it when we are afraid, even as grown adults: mom. Think now of your mother. Maybe she lives with you, maybe she is far away, maybe she is sick, or maybe she has already passed and today you miss her. Say her name softly. In the next few minutes we are going to place her in the hands of God, slowly and with no hurry: to thank him for her, to pray for her health and her peace, and to heal whatever needs healing. You do not need to know how. Just stay here and pray with me.

Why we pray for our mother

God himself asked us to honor our mother, and he placed it among his commandments: honor your father and your mother. The Book of Sirach says that whoever honors his mother is like one who lays up treasure. And the last gift Jesus gave from the cross was his own Mother. Seeing her there, beside the disciple he loved, he said: Woman, behold your son. And to the disciple: Behold your mother. Praying for our mother is giving back a little of everything she prayed for us, so many times without our ever knowing it. Let us begin.

Opening prayer

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Lord Jesus, today I bring you my mother. You formed her in her own mother's womb, and you know her better than anyone. I thank you for her life, and for mine, which began in her. I give her to you with everything she is: her joys and her weariness, her faith and her doubts, what she has told me and what she has kept silent out of love. I also give you my failings as a son, as a daughter. What I have not known how to give her, give it to her yourself. And watch over her where I cannot reach.

Psalm twenty-three: the Lord is my shepherd

Let us now pray the psalm of the Good Shepherd, thinking of her. The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside still waters; he restores my soul. He leads me in paths of righteousness for his name's sake. Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me. You prepare a table before me; you anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, and I shall dwell in the house of the Lord forever. Lord, be my mother's shepherd like this: in her good days and in her darkest valleys.

Our Father and Hail Mary

Let us pray together the prayers she may have taught you herself, perhaps at your bedside. Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come, thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

Thank you for her life

Thank you for her life. Lord, I thank you for my mother. For the nights she stayed awake, for the meals she cooked when she was tired, for the times she went without so that I could have. Thank you for her hands, for her voice, and for the way she prayed. Thank you also for everything she taught me without words. And if I ever failed to thank her, receive this prayer today as an embrace that arrives late, but still arrives. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my mother and bless her with your love.

For her health

For her health. Lord, I pray for my mother's health, in body and in soul. If she is sick, hold her up and ease her pain; give wisdom to her doctors and patience to those who care for her. If the years weigh on her, if her memory fails or her strength runs out, may she never lose her peace or her dignity. And if I am the one caring for her, give me tenderness when I am tired and strength when I do not know what to do. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my mother and bless her with your love.

For her peace and her faith

For her peace and her faith. Lord, you know the worries my mother carries in silence. If she feels alone, stay with her. If she is afraid, give her your peace. If she is anxious about one of her children, comfort her and give her rest. Increase her faith, and if she ever drifted away from you, seek her out with the same patience with which she sought me. In her later years, may she never be without your company. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my mother and bless her with your love.

For what needs healing

For what needs healing. Lord, between my mother and me not everything has been easy. There are words that hurt, long silences, and wounds that have not yet closed. If I hurt her, give me the humility to ask her forgiveness while there is still time. If she hurt me, give me a heart able to forgive, and help me see her wounds, because she has them too. Do not let pride be stronger than the love that binds us. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my mother and bless her with your love.

For mothers far away or already gone

For mothers far away or already gone. Lord, I pray for my mother if she lives far from me, in another city or another country: shorten the distance with your love. And I pray for all the mothers who have already left us. If mine is already with you, welcome her into your house and tell her that I still love her. Eternal rest grant unto her, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon her. May she rest in peace. Amen. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my mother and bless her with your love.

A blessing for your mother

Now, if you can, stretch out your hand toward her, even if she is not here, and bless her with the words God gave Moses to bless his people. The Lord bless you and keep you. The Lord make his face to shine upon you and be gracious to you. The Lord lift up his countenance upon you and give you peace. Mom, I bless you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. So many times she blessed you. Today you are the one placing her under the gaze of God.

Under Mary's mantle

Let us now place our mother under the mantle of the Virgin Mary, the Mother Jesus gave us from the cross. We fly to thy protection, O holy Mother of God. Despise not our petitions in our necessities, but deliver us always from all dangers, O glorious and blessed Virgin. Mary, you who stood beside the cross of your Son, stay beside my mother in her hard hours. Mother of all mothers, care for her as she cared for me. Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen.

My mother is yours

Lord, I end this prayer, but I do not stop trusting. My mother is yours before she is mine. Lord, watch over my mother and bless her with your love. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. We will pray for her, and other sons and daughters who pray here will do the same. If your mother has already passed, write that too, and we will remember her.
