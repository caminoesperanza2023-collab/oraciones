Prayer in Times of Crisis: Lord, Calm the Storm in Me

Prayer in times of crisis: if a sudden crisis has turned your life upside down and you do not know what to do, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let Jesus calm the storm.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: Jesus stood up in the storm and said to the sea, Peace, be still. And there was a great calm.

The news came suddenly, and everything I had planned is shaking. You are in this boat with me, even if it seems you are asleep. Calm the wind in my mind and the waves in my heart. Show me the one thing I need to do today. Put good people beside me, and do not let fear make my decisions.

Mary, Star of the Sea, guide me through this storm.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"He woke up and rebuked the wind and said to the sea, 'Peace! Be still!' Then the wind ceased, and there was a great calm" (Mark 4:39). Jesus is in the boat with us even when he seems asleep. We ask him for calm, for the one step we need to take today and for good people at our side, and we entrust ourselves to Mary, Star of the Sea.

What the Church teaches

In the storm, the disciples woke Jesus with a cry: "Teacher, do you not care that we are perishing?" (Mark 4:38). The Church sees in that boat an image of our life and of the Church herself: Jesus does not always take the storm away at once, but he never abandons the boat. Mary is invoked as Stella Maris, Star of the Sea, guide of sailors.
