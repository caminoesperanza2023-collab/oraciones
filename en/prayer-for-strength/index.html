Prayer for Strength: Lord, I Can't Do This Without You

Prayer for strength: if you feel you can't take one more day and no one sees how heavy it is, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let God carry you today.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength.

Lord, I am tired, and I have tried to carry everything alone. Today I put it all in your hands. Give me strength for this day, just for today. When my body gives up, hold me up. When my mind is full of fear, fill it with your peace. When I want to quit, remind me that you have never let me go. I don't ask for an easy road, I ask for you to walk it with me.

Mary, Help of Christians, pray for me and hold my hand.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength; they will soar on wings like eagles" (Isaiah 40:31). Saint Paul heard the same promise: "My grace is sufficient for you, for power is made perfect in weakness" (2 Corinthians 12:9). We ask God for strength for today and entrust ourselves to Mary, Help of Christians.

What the Church teaches

Fortitude is one of the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit: the strength to stay firm in difficulties and to keep going when we would like to give up (Catechism 1808 and 1831). We do not ask for it once and for all, but day by day, like our daily bread.
