Prayer to Saint Monica for a Child Far from God: Nothing Is Far from God

Prayer to saint monica: if your son or daughter has drifted away from God, pray this short Catholic prayer with me to the mother who wept seventeen years for her son, Saint Augustine, and never lost hope.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

A bishop told her: it is impossible that the son of so many tears should be lost. That son became Saint Augustine.

Saint Monica, mother who never stopped hoping, today I place my child in your hands. You know their name and the road they have taken. Ask Jesus to go out to meet them, as He met your son in that garden. May a word, a person or a light touch their heart. And give me patience so I do not grow weary, help me talk less and pray more, and keep the door of my home always open.

Nothing is far from God. Not even my child.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Go in peace: it is impossible that the son of so many tears should be lost" (Saint Augustine, Confessions III, 12). Before she died, Monica said: "Nothing is far from God" (Confessions IX, 11). Watch her full story on our channel.

What the Church teaches

Saint Augustine tells in his Confessions (III, 12) that a bishop said to his mother: "Go in peace: it is impossible that the son of so many tears should be lost." The Church celebrates Saint Monica on August 27, the day before her son, a Doctor of the Church.
