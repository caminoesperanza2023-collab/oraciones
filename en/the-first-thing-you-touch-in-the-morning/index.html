What the video says

Think about what you actually touch first in the morning. Not what you should touch, what you really do. This isn't about guilt. It's about one very concrete thing. Who decides what you think about for the first 10 minutes of your day? If the first thing you open is notifications, your day begins answering what other people decided to send you.

Before you're even fully awake, your head is already working [music] someone else's agenda. St. Joseé Maria gave the opposite moment a name. He called it the heroic minute. The exact instant you get up without negotiating with yourself. And he said that minute set the tone for everything after it.

You don't need an hour of prayer, one line before you unlock anything. Lord, this day is yours. Six words. Then pick up the phone if [music] you want, but let the first thought of the day be one you chose. What's the first thing you touch?
