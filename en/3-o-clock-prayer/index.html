The 3 O'Clock Prayer: The Hour of Mercy Jesus Asked Us to Remember

The 3 o'clock prayer: at three in the afternoon, the hour Jesus died on the cross, stop for one minute and pray the Hour of Mercy prayer with me.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Saint Faustina wrote that at this hour Jesus asked us to remember his Passion and to trust in his mercy.

You expired, Jesus, but the source of life gushed forth for souls, and the ocean of mercy opened up for the whole world. O Blood and Water, which gushed forth from the Heart of Jesus as a fountain of mercy for us, I trust in You.

Jesus, I place in your wounds my sins, my worries, and the people I love. Have mercy on us and on the whole world.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Saint Faustina recorded in her Diary that Jesus asked to be remembered at three o'clock, the hour of his death, imploring mercy for the whole world (Diary 1320, 1572). The prayer "O Blood and Water..." comes from her Diary (187).

What the Church teaches

Jesus died at about the ninth hour, three in the afternoon (Mark 15:34-37). Saint Faustina recorded that he asked for that hour to be honored by meditating on his Passion and imploring mercy, especially for sinners (Diary 1320, 1572). Her canonization in 2000 and the institution of Divine Mercy Sunday by Saint John Paul II are the Church's recognition of this devotion; as private revelation it serves the Gospel, never replaces it (Catechism 67).
