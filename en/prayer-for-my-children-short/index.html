Prayer for My Children: Place Them in God's Hands Today

Prayer for my children: if one of your children is on your heart tonight, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place them in the hands of Jesus. A mother's and grandmother's prayer for protection, peace, honest work, and the return to faith of children who have drifted away.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Lord Jesus, today I place my children in your hands.

You know them better than I do. Where I cannot reach, your love can. Give them peace, honest work, and good people at their side. And when I no longer have the strength to guide them, speak to their hearts yourself.

Your Word promises: great shall be the peace of your children. Today I believe that promise, even before I see it. I leave everything in your heart, Lord.

Amen.

What the Bible says

God's promise in Isaiah 54:13: "Great shall be the peace of your children."

What the Church teaches

The promise "great shall be the peace of your children" comes from Isaiah 54:13. God spoke it to his people when they felt abandoned, and the Church prays it for families too. Praying for our children is not controlling their lives; it is entrusting them to the One who loves them even more than we do. Saint Monica prayed for years for her son Augustine, who returned to the faith and became a saint.
