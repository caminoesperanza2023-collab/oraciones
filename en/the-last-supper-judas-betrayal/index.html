The Last Supper: The Moment of Betrayal

Jesus knew who would betray Him, yet He still broke bread with him. The Last Supper holds a lesson of love few notice.

Watch the video

What the video says

At the last supper, Jesus knew exactly who was about to betray him, and he still washed his feet. That detail changes everything. It was the night before his death. Jesus took bread, broke it, and said, "This is my body." He took the cup, "This is my blood." Then he looked at the twel and said quietly, "One of you will betray me." Judas asked, "Is it I, Lord?" Jesus answered, "You have said it." And Judas went out.

It was night. Jesus loved until the very end, [music] even the one who betrayed him. Is there someone you are struggling to forgive?
