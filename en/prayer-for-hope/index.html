Prayer for Hope: Lord, Light the Flame in Me Again

Prayer for hope: if you feel that nothing is going to change and you have stopped hoping, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let God light the flame in you again.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in him.

Lord, I am tired of waiting for things to get better. Do not let my heart grow cold. Light a small flame of hope in me again, even if it is only enough for today. Help me see the good things you are still doing and the doors you are still opening. And when I cannot hope for myself, let me lean on the faith of others.

Mary, Mother of hope, keep my lamp burning.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit" (Romans 15:13). Hope is not pretending everything is fine: it is trusting that God is still at work. We ask him for enough light for today and entrust ourselves to Mary, Mother of hope.

What the Church teaches

Hope is one of the three theological virtues, together with faith and charity: it is God's gift that makes us desire heaven and trust in his promises, relying not on our own strength but on the grace of the Holy Spirit (Catechism 1817). That is why we can hope even when we cannot see how things will turn out.
