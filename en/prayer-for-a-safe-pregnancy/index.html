Prayer for a Safe Pregnancy: Lord, Protect Mother and Baby

Prayer for a safe pregnancy: if you are expecting a baby, or someone you love is, pray this short Catholic prayer with me for the mother and the child.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says: you formed my inmost being; you knit me together in my mother's womb.

Lord, giver of life, I place this mother and this baby in your hands. Protect them every day of the pregnancy. Give the mother rest, strength and peace, and take away every fear. Guide the doctors, and grant a safe and happy birth. May this child grow healthy and loved, and may this family always know you.

Virgin Mary, mother of Jesus, hold them under your mantle.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"You formed my inmost being; you knit me together in my mother's womb" (Psalm 139:13). We ask God, the giver of life, to protect mother and baby, guide the doctors and grant a safe birth, under the mantle of the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

The Church teaches that human life must be respected and protected from the moment of conception, because every child is willed and loved by God (Catechism 2270). Psalm 139 sings of God forming us in our mother's womb, and in the Visitation, Elizabeth's child leapt for joy when Mary, carrying Jesus, came to greet her (Luke 1:41). Saint Gerard Majella is invoked by many families as the patron of expectant mothers.
