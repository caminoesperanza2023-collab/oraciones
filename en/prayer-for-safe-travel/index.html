Prayer for Safe Travel: Lord, Protect Us on the Road

Prayer for safe travel: if you or someone you love is about to travel by road, by air or by sea, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place the journey in God's hands.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says: the Lord will guard your going out and your coming in, now and forever.

Lord, I place this journey in your hands. Go before us on every road, keep the driver alert and every vehicle safe. Protect us from accidents, bad weather and every danger we cannot see. Give us patience on the way, and bring us home in peace to the people who are waiting for us.

Saint Raphael, who guided Tobias, walk beside us. Virgin Mary, cover us with your mantle.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"The Lord will guard your going out and your coming in, now and forever" (Psalm 121:8). We ask God for protection on the road, with the Archangel Raphael, who guided Tobias on his journey (Tobit 5), and the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

Psalm 121 is called a song of ascents: the pilgrims sang it on their way to Jerusalem, trusting that "the Lord will guard your going out and your coming in." In the Book of Tobit, the Archangel Raphael walks beside young Tobias on his journey and brings him home safely (Tobit 5-12), and the Church teaches that each believer has an angel as protector and guide (Catechism 336). Praying before a trip is a simple way to entrust it to God.
