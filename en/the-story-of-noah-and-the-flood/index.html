The Story of Noah: The Man Who Obeyed God When the World Laughed

God looked at the world He created — and His heart broke. Violence and corruption had filled the earth. But one man still walked with God. His name was Noah.

For 100 years, Noah built an ark with no sea in sight. His neighbors mocked him. He kept building. When the flood came, only the ark floated. When the waters receded, God placed a rainbow in the sky — His promise: never again.

Noah is the pattern of every believer who obeys God when the world calls them crazy.
