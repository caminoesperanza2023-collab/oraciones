Prayer for God's Forgiveness: Lord, I'm Sorry, Have Mercy on Me

Prayer for god's forgiveness: if you carry guilt and think God can't forgive you, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and come back to his mercy.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it clearly: if we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us.

Lord, you know what I did. I don't need to explain it to you. I am sorry. I am sorry for the harm I caused and for the good I failed to do. I won't hide from you anymore. Wash me, Lord, and give me a clean heart. Give me the courage to go to confession, and the humility to repair what I can. And help me forgive myself, as you forgive me.

Virgin Mary, refuge of sinners, take my hand and bring me back to your Son.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Saint John writes: "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us" (1 John 1:9). This prayer does not replace confession: it prepares you for it. God's mercy is bigger than any sin, and we entrust our return to the Virgin Mary, refuge of sinners.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism teaches that there is no sin so serious that the Church cannot forgive it, as long as there is true repentance (Catechism 982). For serious sins, the ordinary way to receive that forgiveness is the sacrament of Confession (Catechism 1446, 1456). If it has been years, don't be afraid: tell the priest at the start, and he will help you.
