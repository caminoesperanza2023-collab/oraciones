What the Church teaches

Your guardian angel is not a poetic idea — it is the constant teaching of the Catholic Church, rooted in Scripture. Jesus Himself said of the little ones: 'their angels in heaven always see the face of my Father' (Matthew 18:10). The story of Saint John Bosco and Grigio, the mysterious grey dog that appeared for years exactly when danger threatened him, reminds us that God's protection is real and personal. The Catechism teaches that from infancy to death, human life is surrounded by the watchful care and intercession of angels.
