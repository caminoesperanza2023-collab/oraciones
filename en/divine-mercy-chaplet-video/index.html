Text of the prayer

There is a prayer Jesus himself promised would obtain mercy for the whole world and it takes less than 10 minutes to pray. In the 1930s, Jesus appeared to a humble Polish nun St. Fina Kowalsska and gave her the divine mercy chaplet. Prayed on ordinary rosary beads.

It offers the father the body, blood, soul, and divinity of his beloved son in atonement for our sins and those of the whole world for the sake of his sorrowful passion. We beg mercy on us and on everyone. Jesus promised extraordinary graces to those who pray it.

Especially at 3:00 in the afternoon, the hour of his death on the cross and at the side of the dying. He told Fstaina, "The soul that trusts in my mercy will not be disappointed. When guilt tells you that you are too far gone, the chaplet answers, his mercy is greater.

No sin is bigger than the ocean of divine mercy." Jesus, I trust in you.
