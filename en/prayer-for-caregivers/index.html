Prayer for Caregivers: Lord, Give Strength to These Tired Hands

Prayer for caregivers: if you care for a sick parent, spouse or relative, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask God for strength, patience and peace.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said: whatever you did for one of the least of my brothers, you did for me.

Lord, you see how tired I am. You see the nights without sleep, the worry I hide and the love nobody notices. Give me strength for one more day. Give me patience when I run out, and peace when I feel guilty. Take care of me, so that I can take care of them. And remind me that in them, I am caring for you.

Virgin Mary, you stood at the foot of the cross: stay beside me.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Jesus said: "Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of mine, you did for me" (Matthew 25:40). This prayer is for those who look after the sick and the elderly day after day, often without anyone noticing, and we entrust them to the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

Visiting and caring for the sick is one of the corporal works of mercy, and Jesus identifies himself with those who suffer: "I was sick and you visited me" (Matthew 25:36; Catechism 2447). Caring for elderly or sick parents is also part of the Fourth Commandment (Catechism 2218). Caregivers need care too: asking for help, resting and speaking with a priest or a doctor is not selfish, and it is not a lack of love.
