Prayer When You Feel Alone: Jesus Is With You Now

Prayer when you feel alone: if the house is too quiet and no one calls, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and remember that Jesus is with you right now.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said, I am with you always, until the end of the age. You are not forgotten.

Lord Jesus, you know the silence of my home and the weight of my heart. Sit with me today. Fill the empty chair with your presence. Heal the loneliness I do not tell anyone about, and send me one person who needs me as much as I need them.

Virgin Mary, you stayed at the foot of the cross.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"I am with you always, until the end of the age" (Matthew 28:20). The Lord is close to the brokenhearted (Psalm 34:18), and Mary stayed at the foot of the cross (John 19:25).

What the Church teaches

Loneliness is not a sign that God has left us. Jesus promised: "I am with you always, until the end of the age" (Matthew 28:20), and Scripture says the Lord is close to the brokenhearted (Psalm 34:18). Jesus himself knew abandonment in Gethsemane and on the cross, so he understands it from within. The Church invites us to bring our loneliness to prayer, to the Eucharist, and to the community of the parish, where Christ is present in our brothers and sisters (Catechism 2559, 1396).
