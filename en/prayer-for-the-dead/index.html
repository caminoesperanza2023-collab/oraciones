Prayer for the Dead: Pray With Me for Your Loved Ones (Eternal Rest Grant Unto Them)

PRAYER FOR THE DEAD, prayed together: say the name of your loved one and pray with me, slowly and with no hurry, for about ten minutes. For November, the month of the faithful departed, for an anniversary, or for any day the memory feels heavy.

We pray Psalm 130, Out of the Depths, the Our Father, the Hail Mary, the Hail Holy Queen and Eternal Rest, with five intentions: for our parents and grandparents, for a husband, a wife, a brother or sister, for the children who died before their parents, for those who died alone or suddenly, and for the forgotten souls. You do not need to know the prayers by heart: I will lead and you simply follow.

Full text of the prayer

Pray with me for your loved ones

There are names you can no longer call on the phone, but you can still carry them to God. Think now of the person you lost: your mother, your father, your husband, your wife, a brother, a sister, a friend. Say their name quietly. For the next few minutes we are going to pray for them together, slowly, with no hurry at all. You do not need to know how. Just stay here and pray with me. Your love did not end on the day they died. Today that love becomes a prayer.

Why Catholics pray for the dead

The Church has prayed for her dead since the earliest centuries. The Second Book of Maccabees calls this a holy and pious thought, and the Catechism teaches that our prayer can truly help them. That is why November is the month of the faithful departed. We do not know everything that happens after death, and we are not going to make it up. What we do know is that God is merciful, that death does not break the communion of saints, and that no love offered to God is ever lost. So let us begin.

Opening prayer

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Lord Jesus, you wept at the tomb of your friend Lazarus. You know this pain. Today we bring you the people we love who are no longer with us. We bring you the words we never got to say, the embraces we still owed them, and the forgiveness that came too late. We bring you the last conversation, the hospital room, the phone call we will never forget. Receive all of it, Lord, and have mercy on them and on us.

Psalm 130: Out of the depths

We pray now the psalm the Church has always prayed for her dead. Out of the depths I cry to you, O Lord; Lord, hear my voice. Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my pleading. If you, O Lord, should keep a record of sins, Lord, who could stand? But with you there is forgiveness, and for this we revere you. My soul waits for the Lord, and in his word I hope. My soul longs for the Lord more than watchmen for the morning. More than watchmen for the morning, let Israel hope in the Lord. For with the Lord there is mercy, and with him is plentiful redemption, and he will redeem Israel from all its sins. Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them. May they rest in peace. Amen.

Our Father and Hail Mary

We pray in the words Jesus taught us, and we ask his Mother to stay close to the ones we love. Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come, thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

For our parents and grandparents

We pray first for our parents and our grandparents. Lord, they gave us life. They taught us to speak, to work, and perhaps to pray. Thank you for their tired hands and for everything they carried in silence for our sake. Forgive what they did not know how to do better, as we want to forgive it too, and give them now in your house the rest they earned so well. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. For the children who died before their parents

We pray for the sons and daughters who died before their parents. Lord, there is no deeper pain, and your Mother knows it, because she stood at the foot of the cross. We do not ask you to explain what cannot be explained. We ask you to hold those children very close to you, and to embrace every father and every mother who prays today with a broken heart. For those who died alone or suddenly

We pray for those who died alone, suddenly, or without a chance to say goodbye. Lord, no one was with them in the last hour, but you were. For those who died in a hospital without their family, for those who died in an accident, and for those who lost all hope in the middle of their pain, we ask for your mercy, which is greater than anything we know. For the forgotten souls

We pray for the souls no one remembers anymore. Lord, there are graves no one visits and names no one says. For all those souls, and for those most in need of your mercy, we offer this prayer. May not one of them be left without someone to pray for them. And when our own hour comes, may we not lack someone to pray for us. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. The Church's prayer for the faithful departed

O God, Creator and Redeemer of all the faithful, grant to the souls of your servants the remission of all their sins, that through our humble prayers they may obtain the mercy they have always desired. You who live and reign for ever and ever. Amen. Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen.

The hope of seeing them again

Listen now to the words of Jesus to Martha, who was weeping for her brother. I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, even if he dies, will live. And listen to the promise in the Book of Revelation: God will wipe every tear from their eyes, and there will be no more death, no more mourning, no more pain. That is why we grieve, but not as people who have no hope. Lord, comfort those of us who are still here. Give us peace when the memories come, and bring us all together one day with you.
