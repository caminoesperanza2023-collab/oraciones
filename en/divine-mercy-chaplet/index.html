Divine Mercy Chaplet: Pray It With Me Now, Bead by Bead (Spoken, 3 O'Clock Prayer)

DIVINE MERCY CHAPLET, prayed together: pray the complete Chaplet of Divine Mercy with me right now, bead by bead, in about eleven minutes, for you, for your family and for the whole world. Perfect for the 3 o'clock Hour of Mercy, for a novena, or whenever you need to trust Jesus again.

Before each decade we pause for one intention: sinners and our own hearts, our families, the sick and the lonely, the dying, and the holy souls in purgatory with the whole world. Keep your rosary in your hands, or simply count on your fingers.

Saint Faustina Kowalska recorded in her Diary that Jesus taught her this prayer in 1935 (Diary 476), and the closing prayer "Eternal God, in whom mercy is endless" also comes from her Diary (950). Saint John Paul II canonized her in 2000 and gave the whole Church Divine Mercy Sunday. As an approved private devotion, it helps us live the Gospel of Christ's mercy; it never replaces it (Catechism 66-67).

Full text of the prayer

Pray with me now

Pray the Divine Mercy Chaplet with me right now. Whatever you are carrying today, a worry, a sin you regret, or someone you love who is far from God, bring it here. In the next few minutes we will pray the whole chaplet together, bead by bead, for you, for your family, and for the whole world. If you have a rosary, take it in your hands. If not, your fingers are enough.

The prayer of mercy

Saint Faustina Kowalska, a Polish religious sister, wrote in her Diary that Jesus taught her this prayer in 1935. The Church received it as a devotion of trust, and Saint John Paul the Second gave the whole Church Divine Mercy Sunday. Many pray it at three in the afternoon, the hour Jesus died on the cross. Let us begin.

Closing prayer

Eternal God, in whom mercy is endless and the treasury of compassion inexhaustible, look kindly upon us and increase Your mercy in us, that in difficult moments we might not despair nor become despondent, but with great confidence submit ourselves to Your holy will, which is Love and Mercy itself. Amen.
