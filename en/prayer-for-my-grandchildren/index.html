Prayer for My Grandchildren: Lord, Protect Them Always

Prayer for my grandchildren: whether they live close or far away, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your grandchildren in God's hands today.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says that grandchildren are the crown of the elderly, and that God's mercy lasts from generation to generation.

Lord Jesus, today I place my grandchildren in your hands. Protect them on every road, in every school, and in every screen they look at. Keep them from evil, from bad company and from despair. Give them friends who lead them to you, and let them never forget how much they are loved.

Saint Joachim and Saint Anne, grandparents of Jesus, pray for them. Virgin Mary, cover them with your mantle, and let the faith I received reach their hearts.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Grandchildren are the crown of the elderly" (Proverbs 17:6), and God's mercy lasts "from generation to generation" (Luke 1:50). We ask Saint Joachim and Saint Anne, the grandparents of Jesus, to watch over them.

What the Church teaches

Saint Joachim and Saint Anne, the parents of the Virgin Mary, are honored by the Church as the grandparents of Jesus (feast on July 26). Pope Francis instituted the World Day for Grandparents and the Elderly in 2021, recalling that grandparents hand on the faith. "Grandchildren are the crown of the elderly" (Proverbs 17:6), and the Church teaches that the family is the first school of prayer (Catechism 2685).
