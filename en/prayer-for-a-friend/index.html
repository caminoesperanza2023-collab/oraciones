Prayer for a Friend: Lord, Take Care of My Friend

Prayer for a friend: if a friend is going through a hard time, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place them in God's hands right now.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Scripture says, a faithful friend is a sturdy shelter, and whoever finds one has found a treasure.

Lord Jesus, you called your disciples your friends. Today I bring you my friend. You know what they are carrying, even what they have not told me. Give them strength, healing and peace. Protect them on every road, and let them feel that they are not alone, because you are with them and so am I.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"A faithful friend is a sturdy shelter; whoever finds one has found a treasure" (Sirach 6:14). Jesus called his disciples friends (John 15:15). Today we pray for strength, healing and peace for the friends we love.

What the Church teaches

Praying for others is called intercession, and it is one of the most Christ-like forms of prayer: Jesus himself intercedes for us before the Father (Catechism 2634-2635). Scripture praises true friendship: "A faithful friend is a sturdy shelter; whoever finds one has found a treasure" (Sirach 6:14), and Jesus said to his disciples, "I have called you friends" (John 15:15). Bringing a friend's name to God is a real act of love.
