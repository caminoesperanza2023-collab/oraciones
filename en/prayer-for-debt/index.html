Prayer for Debt: Lord, I Don't Know How I'm Going to Pay

Prayer for debt: if the bills keep piling up and you can't sleep, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and give that burden to God.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said it: your Father in heaven knows what you need. You don't carry this alone.

Lord, you see the bills on my table. You see the shame I feel and what I haven't told anyone. Today I can't handle this by myself. Give me calm to look at the numbers without fear. Give me honest work and the strength to keep my word. Send me someone who can guide me. Protect my home and my family from this anguish. And don't let any of this take away my peace or my faith in you.

Saint Joseph, you know what it is to work to put bread on the table: pray for me.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Jesus said: "Your heavenly Father knows that you need them all" (Matthew 6:32). This prayer does not promise that the debt will vanish. It asks God for calm, honest work and good advice, and we entrust it to Saint Joseph, who worked to feed his family.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism recalls the old advice: "Pray as if everything depended on God and work as if everything depended on you" (Catechism 2834). Praying does not wipe out a debt overnight, and nobody should promise you that. It does give you calm to take the next step: talk to the people you owe, look for trustworthy advice, and don't take on new debt out of panic.
