Mary's 15 Promises to Those Who Pray the Rosary — #7 Will Amaze You

Mary made 15 promises to those who pray the Rosary faithfully. These are not folklore — they were given to Blessed Alan de la Roche and Saint Louis de Montfort.

Whoever prays the Rosary shall not perish by sword, fire, or flood. They will not die without the sacraments. Sinners will be converted. The lukewarm will grow fervent. And the most powerful promise: their souls will be liberated from purgatory on the Saturday after death.

The Rosary is not superstition. It is a covenant with the Mother of God.

Text of the prayer

Mary made 15 promises to those who pray the rosary faithfully. Here are the most powerful ones. Whoever prays the rosary shall not perish by the sword or fire or flood. They will be protected. They will not die without receiving the sacraments. God will not let them die [music] unprepared.

Sinners will be converted. The just will persevere. The lukewarm will grow fervent. All who recite the rosary will have as their companions in life and death the holy rosary brotherhood. Those who are faithful to it shall not be conquered by any adversity. And the most beautiful promise, their souls will be liberated from purgatory on the Saturday after their death.

The rosary is not superstition. It is a covenant. Start praying it
