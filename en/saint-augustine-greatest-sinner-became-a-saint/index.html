Saint Augustine Prayed to Be Holy — But Not Yet. God Had Other Plans.

He prayed: 'Lord, make me holy — but not yet.' And that prayer ended up changing the entire Church.

Augustine of Hippo was a genius who spent years lost: he had a son outside marriage, joined a sect, chased pleasure and fame in Milan. His mother Saint Monica followed him everywhere, praying without stopping. One night in a garden he heard a voice: 'take up and read.' He opened Paul's letters, read one line, and something broke inside him forever. He was baptized at 33, became a bishop, a theologian, and a Doctor of the Church. And he wrote the most beautiful words in spiritual history: 'You have made us for yourself, O Lord, and our heart is restless until it rests in You.'

If God could reach Augustine, He can reach you.
