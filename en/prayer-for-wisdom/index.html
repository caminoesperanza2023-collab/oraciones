Prayer for Wisdom: Lord, Show Me What to Do

Prayer for wisdom: if you feel confused and you don't know what God wants from you, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask the Holy Spirit for light.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: if any of you lacks wisdom, ask God, and it will be given to you.

Lord, I don't have all the answers, and sometimes I don't even know the right questions. Help me see my life the way you see it. Teach me when to speak and when to stay silent, when to wait and when to act. Guard me from pride and from hasty decisions. And when I can't see the whole road, give me light for the next step.

Holy Spirit, fill me with your gift of wisdom.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives to all generously, and it will be given him" (James 1:5). Like King Solomon, who asked for "a listening heart" (1 Kings 3:9), we ask God for wisdom to see our life as he sees it and light for the next step.

What the Church teaches

Wisdom is the first of the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit (Catechism 1831). It is not the same as being clever: it is seeing things the way God sees them. That is why Solomon did not ask for riches or a long life, but for a listening heart, and God gave him the rest as well (1 Kings 3:11-13).
