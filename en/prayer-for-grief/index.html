Prayer in Grief: Lord, Hold My Broken Heart

Prayer in grief: if you lost someone you love and the pain will not go away, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let God hold your broken heart.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said: blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.

Lord, my heart is broken and the house feels empty. I miss their voice, their laugh, their place at the table. I do not understand, but I come to you. Hold me when I cannot stand. Dry my tears, heal this wound little by little, and teach me to live with hope. Let me feel that love does not end.

Mother of Sorrows, you know this pain: hold my hand.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Jesus said: "Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted" (Matthew 5:4). In this month of November, when the Church remembers the faithful departed, we ask for comfort for those who stay behind, and we entrust our sorrow to Our Lady of Sorrows.

What the Church teaches

Jesus himself wept at the tomb of his friend Lazarus (John 11:35): grief is not a lack of faith. The Church teaches that for those who die in Christ, "life is changed, not ended" (Catechism 1012), and comforting the afflicted is a spiritual work of mercy (Catechism 2447). Mary stood at the cross and knows this pain, and that is why we call her Our Lady of Sorrows. Having a Mass offered for your loved one and talking with a priest can help a great deal; if the sadness does not lift, ask for help too.
