The Ten Commandments: God Did Not Give Them to Control You

God did not give you the Ten Commandments to control you. He gave them to set you free.

Each commandment protects something precious: the first three protect your relationship with God. The fourth protects the family. The fifth protects life. The sixth protects love and fidelity. The seventh through tenth protect truth and the dignity of others.

Jesus expanded them even further: not killing includes not killing with your words or your hatred.

God did not give you the ten commandments [music] to control you. He gave them to set you free. The first three are about God. No other gods, no false idols. Keep the Sabbath holy. The fourth is the bridge. Honor your father and mother. The family is sacred to God.

You shall not kill. And Jesus expanded it. Not even with your words or your hatred. You shall not commit adultery, protect love, family, and fidelity. You shall not steal, bear false witness, or covet. These protect truth and the dignity of others. These are not a cage.

They are the blueprint of a world where love is actually possible. Oh Lord, your commands are older than Moses. They're older than Adam. They are your words, your wisdom, your love for us. Help us to know them, to love them, and to keep them.
