The Memorare Prayer: Remember, O Most Gracious Virgin Mary

The memorare prayer: when you do not know what else to do, pray this ancient Catholic prayer to the Virgin Mary with me and put your need in her hands.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Mary never leaves you alone.

Remember, O most gracious Virgin Mary, that never was it known that anyone who fled to thy protection, implored thy help, or sought thy intercession was left unaided. Inspired by this confidence, I fly unto thee, O Virgin of virgins, my Mother. To thee do I come, before thee I stand, sinful and sorrowful. O Mother of the Word Incarnate, despise not my petitions, but in thy mercy hear and answer me. Amen.

Now tell Mary what you need.

Amen.

What the Bible says

The Memorare (from the Latin "remember") has been prayed since the Middle Ages and was spread by Father Claude Bernard in the 17th century; it is often attributed to Saint Bernard of Clairvaux. It reminds Mary that no one who asked for her help was ever left alone, and asks her, as Mother of the Word made flesh, to hear our petitions.

What the Church teaches

The Church honors Mary as Mother of God and our Mother, and asks her intercession because "she has not laid aside this saving office" (Catechism 969). Mother Teresa of Calcutta used to pray "flying novenas" of nine Memorares in a row when she needed urgent help, and a tenth in thanksgiving.
