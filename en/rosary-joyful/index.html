Holy Rosary Today: Joyful Mysteries Full Rosary Prayed Aloud (Monday & Saturday)

HOLY ROSARY TODAY, JOYFUL MYSTERIES: pray the complete Rosary with me right now, bead by bead, with a voice beside you on every Hail Mary. For Mondays and Saturdays, and for any day you need joy and hope: God draws near, and the Virgin Mary walks with you.

Before each decade we pause for a short meditation: the Annunciation, the Visitation, the Nativity, the Presentation in the Temple and the Finding in the Temple. You don't need to know the prayers by heart: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, or simply count on your fingers.

Full text of the prayer

Pray the Holy Rosary with me

Pray the Holy Rosary with me right now, with the Joyful Mysteries, the mysteries for Monday and Saturday. If you are tired, worried, or out of words, that is all right: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, and if you don't have one, your fingers are enough. Think of one person or one intention, and place it in the hands of the Virgin Mary. Let us begin.

Sign of the Cross and Apostles' Creed

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth; and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord; who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; on the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there he will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy Catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

She is troubled, she asks, and she answers: let it be done to me according to your word. Perhaps God is asking something of you too, something you don't fully understand. Tell him today, fear and all: here I am, Lord, let your will be done in my life. Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. When she hears Mary's greeting, Elizabeth is filled with the Holy Spirit and says: blessed are you among women. Who do you need to go and see? Someone is alone and waiting for your call. Ask for a heart that goes out of itself, and bring that person a little joy. Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Third Mystery: The Nativity

The third joyful mystery: the birth of Jesus in Bethlehem. There was no room for them at the inn. Mary gives birth to her Son and lays him in a manger. The first to hear are poor shepherds keeping watch at night. God does not wait until everything is in order: he comes to the humble. Open to him today the poorest part of your life, the part that makes you ashamed, and let him be born there. Fourth Mystery: The Presentation in the Temple

The fourth joyful mystery: the Presentation of Jesus in the Temple. Mary and Joseph bring Jesus to the Temple to offer him to God. Old Simeon, who has waited for years, takes him in his arms and blesses God. And he tells Mary that a sword will pierce her soul. Offer to God the people you love, and place them in his hands. And learn from Simeon and Anna to wait with patience, even as the years go by. Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Fifth Mystery: The Finding in the Temple

The fifth joyful mystery: the Finding of Jesus in the Temple. On the way back from Jerusalem, Mary and Joseph cannot find Jesus, and they search for him in anguish for three days. At last they find him in the Temple, among the teachers. If you feel you have lost God, or if someone in your family has drifted away from him, do not stop searching. Mary knows that anguish: ask her to stay with you until you find him. Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Hail Holy Queen and closing

Hail, holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile, show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen. Let us pray. O God, whose only begotten Son, by his life, death and resurrection, has purchased for us the rewards of eternal life, grant, we beseech thee, that while meditating on these mysteries of the most holy Rosary of the Blessed Virgin Mary, we may imitate what they contain and obtain what they promise, through the same Christ our Lord. Amen. Thank you for praying with me. May the Virgin Mary watch over you and everyone you love. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
