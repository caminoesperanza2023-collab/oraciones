Prayer for My Father: Lord, Bless and Protect Him

Prayer for my father: if your father is far away, sick, tired or already in heaven, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place him today in God's hands and under the protection of Saint Joseph.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus taught us to call God Father, the Father who runs to embrace the son who comes home.

Lord, today I place my father in your hands. Thank you for his life, for his work, and for every sacrifice he made for us. Give him health, strength and peace; heal whatever is wounded between us, and if he has already gone home, grant him eternal rest with you.

Saint Joseph, guardian of the Holy Family, protect him and guide him. May he never lack God's love, and may I honor him and care for him every day.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Jesus taught us to call God Father: the Father who sees his son coming home from far away, runs and embraces him (Luke 15:20). Thank God for his life and his work, ask for his health, strength and peace, healing for the wounds of the family and, if he has gone home, eternal rest.

What the Church teaches

In the parable of the prodigal son, the father sees his son from far away, is filled with compassion, runs and embraces him before he says a word (Luke 15:20): this is the Father Jesus reveals to us. The fourth commandment, "Honor your father and your mother" (Exodus 20:12), asks for gratitude and care also when our parents grow old (Sirach 3:12; Catechism 2218). And Saint Joseph, guardian of the Holy Family, is the patron of fathers.
