Prayer of Thanksgiving: Lord, Today I Only Came to Say Thank You

Prayer of thanksgiving: if you want to stop and thank God today, pray this short Catholic prayer with me, without asking for anything.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Saint Paul wrote it: give thanks in all circumstances. Not only when everything goes well.

Lord, today I'm not here to ask you for anything. I came to say thank you. Thank you for waking me up this morning. Thank you for the people who love me. Thank you for the bread on my table. Thank you for all the times you lifted me up and I didn't even notice. Thank you also for what I still don't understand. Forgive me for remembering you only when I need something. Everything I have comes from your hands.

Virgin Mary, you sang of the greatness of the Lord: teach me to give thanks.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Ten men were healed and only one came back, and Jesus asked: "Where are the other nine?" (Luke 17:17). Saint Paul tells us to give thanks in all circumstances (1 Thessalonians 5:18). Today we simply say thank you, and we learn it from the Virgin Mary.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism teaches that every event and every need can become an offering of thanksgiving (Catechism 2638), and the word Eucharist itself means thanksgiving. That is why giving thanks is not only for good days: Saint Paul asks for it in all circumstances (1 Thessalonians 5:18). If today is hard, you can start small: thank God for one thing.
