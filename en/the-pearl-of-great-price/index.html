He Sold everything for One Pearl — And He Knew Exactly What He Was Doing

A merchant found one pearl and sold absolutely everything he owned to buy it. And he wasn't a starry-eyed amateur — he was the greatest pearl expert in his region.

Matthew calls him an «emporos»: a travelling wholesale trader whose entire profession was knowing exactly what a pearl was worth. In the ancient world pearls outranked gold — Pliny the Elder wrote about entire fortunes hanging from a single ear. So when this man liquidates everything, he isn't sacrificing. He's making the deal of his life, and he knows it.

The Parable of the Pearl of Great Price (Matthew 13:45-46) isn't about surrender. It's about recognition. In the twin parable of the hidden treasure, Matthew adds the word that says it all: he sold everything with joy.
