Noah's Ark in 3D: Its Real Size According to the Bible

Noah's ark at its real size: would it fit on a football field? See it in 3D with the exact measurements God gave Noah.

Pray along with the video

Full text

God gave Noah the exact measurements: three hundred cubits long, fifty wide, and thirty high.

Inside, it had three decks, an opening at the top for light, and just one door in its side.

And when everyone was inside, the Bible says the Lord himself shut the door.

Today, another door is open. Jesus said: I am the door.

What the Bible says

"The length of the ark three hundred cubits, its breadth fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits" (Genesis 6:15). With a cubit of about 18 inches, that's roughly 450 feet long, longer than a whole football field, and about 45 feet tall, like a four-story building. It had three decks, an opening at the top and one door in its side (Genesis 6:16), and "the Lord shut him in" (Genesis 7:16).

What the Church teaches

Genesis 6:15 gives the measurements: three hundred cubits long, fifty wide and thirty high. With a cubit of about 18 inches, that's roughly 450 × 75 × 45 feet. Saint Peter sees in the Ark a figure of the baptism that saves us (1 Peter 3:20-21), and Jesus says: "I am the door; if anyone enters by me, he will be saved" (John 10:9).
