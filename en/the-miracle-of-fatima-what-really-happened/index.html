The Miracle of Fatima: What Really Happened

70,000 people watched the sun dance in the sky. The miracle of Fatima is one of history's most witnessed events.

Watch the video

What the video says

On October 13, 1917, 70,000 people witnessed something that science cannot explain. And the Catholic Church confirmed it as a miracle. Three shepherd children in Fatima, Portugal, had seen the Virgin Mary six times. She told them to pray the rosary for peace. Wet clothes dried instantly.

The mud vanished. 70,000 witnesses. One miracle. Our Lady of Fatima said, "Pray the rosary every day." Are you listening?
