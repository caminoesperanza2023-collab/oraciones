Holy Rosary Today: Sorrowful Mysteries Full Rosary Prayed Aloud (Tuesday & Friday), Pray With Me

HOLY ROSARY TODAY, SORROWFUL MYSTERIES: pray the complete Rosary with me right now, bead by bead, with a voice beside you on every Hail Mary. For Tuesdays and Fridays, and for any day you need to bring your pain to the cross of Jesus and into the hands of the Virgin Mary.

Before each decade we pause for a short meditation: the Agony in the Garden, the Scourging at the Pillar, the Crowning with Thorns, the Carrying of the Cross and the Crucifixion. You don't need to know the prayers by heart: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, or simply count on your fingers.

Full text of the prayer

Pray the Holy Rosary with me

Pray the Holy Rosary with me right now, with the Sorrowful Mysteries, the mysteries for Tuesday and Friday. If you are tired, worried, or out of words, that is all right: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, and if you don't have one, your fingers are enough. Think of one person or one intention, and place it in the hands of the Virgin Mary. Let us begin.

Sign of the Cross and Apostles' Creed

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth; and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord; who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; on the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there he will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy Catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Second Mystery: The Scourging at the Pillar

The second sorrowful mystery: the scourging at the pillar. Jesus is bound and beaten, and he does not answer with hatred. He carries in his body the pain we could never carry. Think now of someone who is suffering in their body: a sick person in your family, someone in a hospital, or you yourself. Say their name in silence, and place them beside Jesus, who understands every pain. Perhaps you too have been wounded by words, looked down on, or made to feel you are worth nothing. Today Jesus looks at you with love. Fourth Mystery: The Carrying of the Cross

On the way he meets his Mother, and Simon of Cyrene helps him carry the weight. What is your cross today? A debt, an illness, a child you worry about. Jesus does not ask you to carry it alone. Fifth Mystery: The Crucifixion

The fifth sorrowful mystery: the crucifixion and death of our Lord. From the cross Jesus forgives those who are killing him, and he gives us his Mother: behold your mother. Then he bows his head and dies, out of love for you. Stay for a moment at the foot of the cross, next to Mary. Ask forgiveness for your sins, and offer this decade for those who are dying today, and for your loved ones who have gone before you. Hail Holy Queen and closing

Hail, holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile, show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen. Let us pray. O God, whose only begotten Son, by his life, death and resurrection, has purchased for us the rewards of eternal life, grant, we beseech thee, that while meditating on these mysteries of the most holy Rosary of the Blessed Virgin Mary, we may imitate what they contain and obtain what they promise, through the same Christ our Lord. Amen. Thank you for praying with me. May the Virgin Mary watch over you and everyone you love. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
