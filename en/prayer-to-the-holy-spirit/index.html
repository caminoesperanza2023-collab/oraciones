Prayer to the Holy Spirit Before Any Decision

Before you make your next decision, pause and pray this with me.

Come, Holy Spirit, fill my heart with your light and your peace. Quiet the noise within me so I can recognize your gentle voice. Give me wisdom to see what is true and courage to follow it. Heal the fear that makes me rush, hide, or lose hope.

A short Catholic prayer to the Holy Spirit for guidance, wisdom and peace: for work, for a hard conversation, for a choice you cannot see clearly. Save it and come back to it whenever the path is unclear.

Text of the prayer

Before you make your next decision, pause and pray this with me. Come, Holy Spirit, fill my heart with your light and your peace. Quiet the noise within me so I can recognize your gentle voice. Guide my words, my choices, and every person I meet today.

Heal the fear that makes me rush, hide, or lose hope. Awaken your gifts in me: understanding, strength, reverence, and holy wonder. Teach me to love patiently, forgive freely, and serve without seeking praise. When the path is unclear, remind me that God has not abandoned me.

Take my plans, purify my intentions, and lead me where grace is waiting. Stay with me today, Holy Spirit, in silence, work, and conversation. Make my life a witness to Jesus. Amen. Save this prayer for later.

What the Church teaches

A short note on this prayer 🕊️ The Church has always turned to the Holy Spirit before deciding anything that matters. Councils open with it. Popes are elected under it. The old Latin hymn Veni Sancte Spiritus asks for exactly what this prayer asks: light, rest for the weary, and the melting of what is frozen in us. Notice what the prayer does not ask for. It does not ask for the answer. It asks for a quiet heart, so the answer can be recognized when it comes. That is the Catholic understanding of discernment: not a lightning bolt, but a slow clearing of the noise, until the true thing looks obvious. The seven gifts named in Isaiah 11 are behind these lines: wisdom, understanding, counsel, fortitude, knowledge, piety, and fear of the Lord. Confirmation gave them to you. Prayer wakes them up. So before the meeting, the message, the decision you keep postponing: thirty seconds, this prayer, and then act. What decision are you bringing to the Holy Spirit today? You do not have to say it. Just a 🕊️ below, and we pray for each other.
