Prayer for the Sick: Lord, Lay Your Healing Hand on Them

Prayer for the sick: if someone you love is sick, or if you are the one who is suffering, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place them in the hands of Jesus, healer of body and soul.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

God's Word says: the prayer of faith will save the sick person.

Lord Jesus, healer of body and soul, today I bring you this person I love so much. You touched the lepers and raised up the paralyzed man; now lay your hand on them. Ease their pain, restore their health if it is your will, give wisdom to their doctors and strength to those who care for them.

And when the night feels long, stay by their side. Let them feel your peace, and never lose hope. Only say the word, Lord, and they shall be healed.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"The prayer of faith will save the sick person" (James 5:15). Ask with trust: ease their pain, restore their health if it is God's will, give wisdom to their doctors and strength to those who care for them.

What the Church teaches

The Church has always prayed for the sick. James 5:14-15 says: "Is anyone among you sick? He should summon the presbyters of the church," and from this comes the Sacrament of the Anointing of the Sick. If your loved one is seriously ill, ask your parish priest to visit: it is not only for the end of life, it is Christ's strength for body and soul (Catechism 1514).
