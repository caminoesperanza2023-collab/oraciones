Prayer for My Mother: Lord, Bless and Protect Her

Prayer for my mother: if your mother is far away, sick, tired or already in heaven, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place her today in God's hands and under the mantle of the Virgin Mary.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

From the cross, Jesus said to the beloved disciple, behold, your mother.

Lord, today I place my mother in your hands. Thank you for her life, for her sleepless nights, and for every prayer she said for me. Give her health, strength and peace; comfort her in her sorrows, and if she has already gone home, grant her eternal rest with you.

Virgin Mary, our Mother, cover her with your mantle. May she never lack love or company, and may I honor her and care for her every day.

Amen.

What the Bible says

From the cross, Jesus gave us his own Mother: "Behold, your mother" (John 19:27). Thank God for her life, ask for her health, strength and peace, and if she has gone home, eternal rest.

What the Church teaches

From the cross, Jesus entrusted his Mother to the beloved disciple: "Behold, your mother" (John 19:27). The Church sees in that moment that Mary is the Mother of all believers (Catechism 964). And the fourth commandment, "Honor your father and your mother" (Exodus 20:12), flows from gratitude to those who gave us life and lasts our whole life (Catechism 2215).
