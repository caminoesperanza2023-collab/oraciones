Psalm 23 Explained: 'The Lord Is My Shepherd' Verse by Verse

Three thousand years ago, a shepherd-king wrote the most comforting words in the Bible. 'Even though I walk through the valley, I will fear no evil — for You are with me.'

Text of the prayer

3,000 years ago, a shepherd king wrote the most comforting words in the entire Bible. You've probably heard the first line, "The Lord is my shepherd. I shall not want." David wrote Psalm 23 knowing exactly what it means to protect a helpless flock. Even though I walk through [music] the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me.

Not because the valley disappears, because he walks through it with you. You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies. [music] God doesn't just save you from danger. He blesses you in the middle of it. Whatever you're walking through today, he is your shepherd,
