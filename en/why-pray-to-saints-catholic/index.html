Why Do Catholics Pray to Saints? The Answer Non-Catholics Never Expect

Why do Catholics pray to saints if God is the only one who answers prayer? This is one of the most common objections to Catholicism — and the biblical answer surprises people every time.

Catholics do not worship saints. They ask for intercession — just as you'd ask a friend to pray for you. In Revelation 5, the saints in heaven offer golden bowls of incense: the prayers of the faithful on earth.

The saints are not dead. Jesus said: God is the God of the living. If your grandmother prayed for you in life, why would she stop now that she is with God?
