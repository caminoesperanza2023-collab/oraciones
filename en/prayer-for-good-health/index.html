Prayer for Good Health: Lord, Bless My Body and My Soul

Prayer for good health: if you are worried about your health or the health of someone you love, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your body in God's hands.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus went through every town healing the sick, and he still says to you: take heart, I am with you.

Lord, I thank you for the gift of my life and for every breath. I place my body in your hands: my heart, my mind, every part of me. Keep me healthy, and if I am sick, give me strength and healing according to your will. Give wisdom to my doctors and patience to those who care for me. Free me from fear while I wait for results, and teach me to rest in you.

Virgin Mary, health of the sick, cover me with your mantle.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Jesus went through all the towns and villages, healing every disease and sickness" (Matthew 9:35). This prayer does not replace your doctor: it asks God to bless the care you receive. We entrust our health to the Virgin Mary, health of the sick.

What the Church teaches

The Church has a sacrament for the sick: the Anointing of the Sick. It is not only for the dying; it can be received by anyone facing a serious illness, an operation or the weakness of old age (Catechism 1514-1515). Ask your parish priest.
