Prayer for Humility: Lord, Free Me from Pride

Prayer for humility: if your pride has hurt the people you love and you want to change, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask Jesus for a humble heart.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.

Lord Jesus, meek and humble of heart, look at my heart. You know how hard it is for me to say I was wrong. You know how much I need to be right, to be seen, to win. Free me from that pride that leaves me alone. Teach me to listen more than I speak, to ask for forgiveness without excuses, and to serve without waiting for applause.

Virgin Mary, humble servant of the Lord, pray for me.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble" (James 4:6). Jesus himself said: "Learn from me, for I am meek and humble of heart" (Matthew 11:29). We ask him to free us from the pride that isolates us, and we entrust ourselves to Mary, the humble servant of the Lord.

What the Church teaches

For the saints, humility is the foundation of every other virtue: Saint Augustine said that it is the first, the second and the third step of the Christian life. The Catechism calls it the foundation of prayer (Catechism 2559), and pride the first of the capital sins (Catechism 1866). The Litany of Humility is a beautiful prayer to continue with.
