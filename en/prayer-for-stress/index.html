Prayer for Stress: Lord, I Can't Carry It All Today

Prayer for stress: if you feel too much pressure and you cannot carry it all, stop for one minute and pray this short Catholic prayer with me.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said: come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

Lord, my mind will not stop. Bills, work, family, things I did not finish, things I cannot fix. I have no strength left, Lord. Today I put it all in your hands, one thing at a time. Take what is not mine to carry. Give me calm to do what I can, and peace to leave the rest to you. Slow down my heart and teach me to rest in you.

Mary, Mother of peace, hold my hand.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Come to me, all you who labor and are burdened, and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28). We place in God's hands the bills, the work, the family worries and everything we cannot fix, we ask for calm to do what we can and peace to leave the rest to him, and we ask Mary, Mother of peace, to hold our hand.

What the Church teaches

Jesus does not promise a life without burdens, but he invites us to carry them with him: "my yoke is easy and my burden light" (Matthew 11:30). Saint Paul adds: "have no anxiety at all, but in everything make your requests known to God, and the peace of God will guard your hearts" (Philippians 4:6-7). Prayer does not replace rest or help when stress becomes illness: it opens the heart to receive both.
