What Is the Holy Trinity? Simply Explained

One God in three Persons. The mystery of the Holy Trinity, explained simply for every believer.

Watch the video

What the video says

Three persons, one God. The most profound mystery of the Christian faith, the Holy Trinity, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Not three gods, but one God in three divine persons. Jesus said, "I and the Father are one." At his baptism, all three were present.

The son in the water, the spirit as a dove, the father speaking from heaven. St. Patrick used a shamrock to explain it. The trinity is not a contradiction. It is the mystery of divine love. You were baptized in the name of the father, the son, and the holy spirit.

That trinity lives in you. Matthew 28 verse
