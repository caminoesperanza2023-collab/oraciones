Prayer for Inner Healing: Lord, Heal the Wounds of My Past

Prayer for inner healing: if something from your past still hurts, even after many years, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let Jesus heal the wounds of your heart.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: he heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.

Lord Jesus, you know my whole story, even what I have never told anyone. I bring you the wounds of my past: the words that hurt me, the people who were not there, the mistakes I still carry. Go back with me to those moments and touch them with your love. Free me from bitterness and from fear. Give me the grace to forgive, and to forgive myself. Fill every empty place in my heart with your peace.

Virgin Mary, Mother of mercy, hold my heart while Jesus heals it.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds" (Psalm 147:3). This prayer does not replace confession or professional help when it is needed: it opens your heart so that Jesus can reach the places that still hurt. We entrust our healing to the Virgin Mary, Mother of mercy.

What the Church teaches

The Gospel shows Jesus healing not only bodies but hearts: the Samaritan woman, Peter after his denial, Zacchaeus. The sacraments of Confession and the Eucharist are the ordinary way that healing reaches us, and seeking a counselor for deep wounds is also an act of faith.
