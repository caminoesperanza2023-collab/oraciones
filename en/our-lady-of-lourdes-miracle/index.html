What the video says

In 1858, a poor 14-year-old girl saw a lady in a cave in the south of France. The Church investigated for four years before believing her. Her name was Bernadette Soubirous. The lady called herself the Immaculate Conception. The lady asked her to dig in the earth.

A spring appeared. People have been healed there ever since. The Lourdes Medical Bureau has investigated over 7,000 claimed cures. 70 have been officially declared miraculous. Vittorio Micheli: his hip bone had been destroyed by cancer. After bathing at Lourdes, the bone regenerated. Doctors called it medically inexplicable.

Bernadette did not profit from any of this. She entered a convent and asked to be forgotten. Her body, discovered in the convent after her death, was found incorrupt — after 30 years in the ground. Our Lady of Lourdes, pray for the sick.
