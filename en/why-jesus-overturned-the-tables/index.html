Why Jesus Overturned the Tables — It Was not Blind Anger

It is the only time we see Jesus turn furniture over. And it was not a burst of rage.

John 2 records something telling: before going in, Jesus made a whip out of cords. That takes time. It was not an impulse.

The problem was not that animals were sold either. Pilgrims travelled great distances and could not bring their own. The problem was where. They had taken over the Court of the Gentiles, the one courtyard where a foreigner was allowed to pray, and turned it into a market.
