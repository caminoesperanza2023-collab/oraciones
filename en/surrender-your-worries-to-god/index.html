Surrender Your Worries to God — The Prayer That Brings Peace

Anxiety is the epidemic of our age. But 2000 years ago, Jesus already had the answer: 'Do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself.'

In this video, discover what Scripture says about surrendering worry, how the saints dealt with fear and anxiety, and a simple act of surrender you can do right now.

Watch the video

Text of the prayer

You [music] have been carrying something you were never meant to carry alone. Jesus said it plainly. Come to me all you who are weary and burdened. He was not speaking to the strong. Worry is one of the most exhausting things a human being can do and it is also one of the most useless.

Jesus said in Matthew 6, "Which of you by worrying can add a single hour to your life?" The answer is none. Not one hour, not one minute. Worry cannot change the past. It cannot control the future. It can only steal the present. But Jesus does not just say, "Stop worrying and leave you there." He gives you something to replace it with.

He says, "Look at the birds of the air. They do not sow or reap or store away in barns. And yet your heavenly father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they? He is not saying life will always be easy. He is saying you have a father who knows what you need before you ask.

St. Peter writes, "Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you." The word cast is violent. It is the same word used when they cast lots for Jesus's garment. You do you do not gently hand your worries to God. You throw them.

You fling them. You refuse to keep holding them. Surrender is not weakness. It is the most courageous act of faith a person can make. Right now, name the thing you have been carrying and throw it to God. He can hold it. You cannot.
