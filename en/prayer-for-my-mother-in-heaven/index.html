Prayer for My Mother in Heaven: Lord, Give Her Eternal Rest

Prayer for my mother in heaven: if your mom has passed away and you miss her, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and ask God to give her eternal rest.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: God will wipe away every tear, and death shall be no more, neither mourning nor pain.

Lord, today I place my mother in your arms. Thank you for her life, for her hands that cared for me and for every prayer she said for me. Forgive her faults and receive her in your peace. Eternal rest grant unto her, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon her. And when I miss her, let me feel that she is close, waiting for me in your house.

Virgin Mary, Mother of us all, present her to your Son.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning nor crying nor pain any more" (Revelation 21:4). The love between a mother and her child does not end with death: in Christ we remain united, and our prayer can still help her.

What the Church teaches

The Catechism teaches that from the earliest days the Church has honored the memory of the dead and offered prayers for them, and that our prayer for them is capable not only of helping them, but also of making their intercession for us effective (Catechism 958). Above all, the Church recommends offering the Mass for them (Catechism 1032).
