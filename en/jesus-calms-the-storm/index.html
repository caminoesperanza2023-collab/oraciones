Jesus Calmed the Storm With Only 3 Words — The Sea of Galilee Miracle

The disciples screamed they were about to die. Jesus was asleep in the middle of the storm.

With only three words — 'Peace. Be still.' — the wind stopped instantly and the sea went flat as glass. This is the real story of the moment even the wind and the sea obeyed Jesus.

What the video says

The most [music] experienced fishermen on the lake were screaming that they were going to die. Jesus was asleep and didn't move. You're about to find out what happened when those men who had fished that same sea for years woke him up in terror and why his answer scared them more than the storm itself.

It was night and they were crossing the Sea of Galilee, a lake they knew by heart. Then the sky broke open. Waves so high they filled the boat with water. And Jesus in the back asleep on a cushion as if nothing was happening. They woke him shouting, "Don't you care if we drown?" He stood up slowly.

He looked at the wind. He looked at the waves. And he said only three words. Peace. Be still. The wind stopped. Not gradually, instantly. The sea went flat as glass in the same second. The fishermen stood in silence, staring at each other. And then they asked something no storm had ever made them ask before.

They asked, "Who is this?" that even the wind and the sea obey him. It wasn't their first storm. It was the first time they'd seen something obey a command straight from God.
