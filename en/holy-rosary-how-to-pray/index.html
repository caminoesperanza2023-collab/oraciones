The Holy Rosary: How to Pray It and Why It Changes Everything

The Rosary is not magic or mindless repetition. It's a way of meditating on the most important moments in the life of Christ — holding Mary's hand as you do it.

Saint Bernadette prayed it daily. Saint Dominic called it a spiritual weapon. And it only takes 20 minutes.

Text of the prayer

The rosary is not magic. It's not mindless repetition. It's a way of meditating on the most important moments in the history of salvation. Holding Mary's hand as you do it. Each decade is a scene from the life of Christ, the enunciation, the birth in Bethlehem, the agony in the garden, the resurrection.

And you are there present praying through each one. St. Bernardet prayed it every day. St. Dominic preached it as a spiritual weapon. St. Fainina prayed it before receiving communion. You don't need hours of free time. A full rosary takes about 20 minutes. One decade takes 2 minutes.

You can pray it in the car, on a walk, before sleep. Our Lady promised that those who pray the rosary faithfully will receive special graces for their soul. Today, pick up a rosary. Start with one decade. Start right

What the Church teaches

This video is for you if you needed a reminder today that you are not alone. Faith is not believing everything will be perfect. It's believing God is present in everything — in what goes well and in what hurts. That changes everything. Saint Paul said it better than anyone ever could: "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." Not all the things I want. All things — including forgiving, waiting, getting back up, and moving forward. If this video found you today, it wasn't by accident. God speaks through the ordinary.
