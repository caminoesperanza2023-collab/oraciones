A Healing Prayer for Anxiety and the Body

When your mind races and your body aches, God brings peace. Pray this healing prayer and rest in the Lord.

Watch the video

Text of the prayer

60 seconds that can calm your heart right now. Jesus healed everyone who came to him and he has not changed. Lord Jesus, touch this tired body and this anxious mind right now. Heal what doctors cannot [music] explain. Restore the peace that worry stole. You said, "I am the Lord who heals you." I claim that promise today.

Let your healing hand cover me from head to toe and bring a peace that passes all understanding. If this prayer found you today, it was not an accident. Type healed if you receive this in faith and send it to someone who is sick or suffering right
