Prayer for Patience: Lord, Calm My Heart When I'm About to Explode

Prayer for patience: if you are about to lose your patience with your family, your work or yourself, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let God calm your heart.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: love is patient. And God is patient with you every single day.

Lord, you know how tired I am and how quickly I get angry. Calm my heart before I say something I will regret. Give me patience with the people I love, with those who hurt me and with the things I cannot change. When I fail, help me ask for forgiveness quickly. And thank you for all the patience you have with me.

Holy Spirit, give me your gift of peace and patience.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Love is patient, love is kind" (1 Corinthians 13:4). Patience is a fruit of the Holy Spirit (Galatians 5:22), not something we achieve alone. We ask God to calm our hearts, to help us wait without despair, and to teach us to treat others with the same patience he has with us.

What the Church teaches

The Church counts patience among the fruits of the Holy Spirit (Catechism 1832). And the old saying of Saint Teresa of Ávila still helps: "Let nothing disturb you, let nothing frighten you; all things pass, God never changes. Patience obtains all things."
