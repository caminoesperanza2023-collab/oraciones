What Happens the Moment You Die?

Death is not the end. Discover what the Catholic faith really teaches about the moment you die.

Watch the video

What the video says

What happens the moment you die? Not in movies, not in opinions. What does the Catholic Church actually teach? The answer might surprise you. At the moment of death, the soul faces what Catholics call the particular judgment. An immediate encounter with [music] Jesus Christ. Not a courtroom, but a meeting.

Those in full union with God enter heaven. Those who need purification pass through purgatory, a final cleansing, not punishment. And those who reject God completely choose hell themselves. God forces no one. Death is not the end. It is a door. Are you ready for what is on the other side?
