Prayer Before Surgery: Lord, Guide the Doctors' Hands

Prayer before surgery: if you or someone you love is about to have an operation, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place it in God's hands right now.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

God says in Isaiah: do not fear, for I am with you; I will strengthen you and help you.

Lord Jesus, divine physician, I place this surgery in your hands. Guide the hands of the doctors and nurses, give them wisdom, calm and precision. Take away the fear, protect the body in every minute of the operation, and grant a quick and peaceful recovery. Whatever happens, let us rest in your love.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Do not fear, for I am with you; I will strengthen you and help you" (Isaiah 41:10). We pray for the doctors and nurses, for calm before the operation and for a peaceful recovery, under the mantle of the Virgin Mary, Health of the Sick.

What the Church teaches

Christ is called the divine physician because he came to heal the whole person, body and soul (Catechism 1503-1505). Sirach teaches us to pray to the Lord and also to give the doctor his place, because God works through their skill (Sirach 38:9-12). The Church also recommends receiving the Anointing of the Sick before a serious operation (Catechism 1515): ask your parish priest. Praying is not instead of medicine, it is trusting God with everything we cannot control.
