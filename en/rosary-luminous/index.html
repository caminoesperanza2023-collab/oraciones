Holy Rosary Today: Luminous Mysteries Full Rosary Prayed Aloud (Thursday), Pray With Me

HOLY ROSARY TODAY, LUMINOUS MYSTERIES: pray the complete Rosary with me right now, bead by bead, with a voice beside you on every Hail Mary. For Thursdays, and for any day you need light on your path: Jesus is the light of the world, and the Virgin Mary walks with you.

Before each decade we pause for a short meditation: the Baptism in the Jordan, the Wedding at Cana, the Proclamation of the Kingdom, the Transfiguration and the Institution of the Eucharist. You don't need to know the prayers by heart: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, or simply count on your fingers.

Full text of the prayer

Pray the Holy Rosary with me

Pray the Holy Rosary with me right now, with the Luminous Mysteries, the mysteries for Thursday. If you are tired, worried, or out of words, that is all right: I will pray, and you only have to follow me. Take your rosary, and if you don't have one, your fingers are enough. Think of one person or one intention, and place it in the hands of the Virgin Mary. Let us begin.

Sign of the Cross and Apostles' Creed

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth; and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord; who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; on the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there he will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy Catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

The heavens open, and the voice of the Father says: this is my beloved Son. Second Mystery: The Wedding at Cana

The second luminous mystery: the wedding at Cana. At a village feast the wine runs out. Mary notices before anyone else and tells Jesus: they have no wine. And she tells the servants: do whatever he tells you. And the water becomes wine. What has run out in your home? Patience, joy, money, tenderness. Third Mystery: The Proclamation of the Kingdom

The third luminous mystery: the proclamation of the Kingdom of God. Jesus goes from town to town and says: the Kingdom of God is at hand, repent and believe in the Gospel. He forgives sinners, heals the sick, and sits at table with those nobody wants. It is never too late to come back. Think of what keeps you far from God, and of who needs your forgiveness today. Ask for a new heart, and if it has been a long time since your last confession, take that step. Fourth Mystery: The Transfiguration

The fourth luminous mystery: the Transfiguration of the Lord. Jesus goes up a mountain with Peter, James and John. His face shines like the sun, and his clothes become white as light. From the cloud the Father says: this is my beloved Son, listen to him. Then they come down the mountain and go back to everyday life. If you are walking in the dark today, ask him to let you see a little of his light, just enough to take the next step. Fifth Mystery: The Institution of the Eucharist

The fifth luminous mystery: the institution of the Eucharist. On the night before he dies, Jesus takes the bread and says: this is my body. He takes the cup and says: this is my blood. And he stays with us forever. Every Mass is that same love. Give thanks for it, and pray for priests, for those who hunger for God, and for anyone who has drifted away from the Church. Hail Holy Queen and closing

Hail, holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile, show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen. Let us pray. O God, whose only begotten Son, by his life, death and resurrection, has purchased for us the rewards of eternal life, grant, we beseech thee, that while meditating on these mysteries of the most holy Rosary of the Blessed Virgin Mary, we may imitate what they contain and obtain what they promise, through the same Christ our Lord. Amen. Thank you for praying with me. May the Virgin Mary watch over you and everyone you love. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
