Prayer for My Family: Lord, Protect My Loved Ones

Prayer for my family: if your family is going through tension, worry or distance, or you simply want to protect it, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your loved ones in God's hands.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Joshua said, as for me and my household, we will serve the Lord.

Lord, today I place my whole family in your hands. Come into our home as you lived in the home of Nazareth. Heal what is wounded, unite what is divided, and protect each one of my loved ones, those who are near and those who are far away.

May our table never lack bread, or forgiveness, or joy. Holy Family of Jesus, Mary and Joseph, watch over us.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"As for me and my household, we will serve the Lord" (Joshua 24:15). Ask Jesus to come into your home as he lived in the home of Nazareth, to heal what is wounded, unite what is divided and protect those near and far.

What the Church teaches

Before all the people, Joshua made a decision for his home: "As for me and my household, we will serve the Lord" (Joshua 24:15). The Church calls the Christian family the "domestic church," the first place where we learn to pray, to forgive and to love (Catechism 1656-1657). That is why we look to the Holy Family of Nazareth as the model for every home.
