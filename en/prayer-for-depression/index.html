Prayer for Depression: Lord, Hold Me When I Can't Go On

Prayer for depression: if sadness won't leave you and you feel you can't go on, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and let Jesus stay with you today.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

The Bible says it: the Lord is close to the brokenhearted, and saves those who are crushed in spirit.

Lord, I don't have the strength to pray much today. Here I am, just as I am. You know this weight on my chest and this darkness I can't explain. I don't ask you to take it all away at once. Stay with me. Give me strength for today, only for today. Give me the courage to ask for help and to talk with someone who can care for me. Remind me that my life has value.

Jesus, you also felt sadness in the garden. Hold me, and do not let go of my hand.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit" (Psalm 34:18). Prayer does not replace medical help: it walks with you while you seek it. If you are in danger or thinking of hurting yourself, call your local emergency number or a crisis line right now. You are not alone.

What the Church teaches

The Church does not see depression as a lack of faith. Saints like Mother Teresa lived long years of inner darkness while still serving God. Prayer and professional help are not opposites: seeking a doctor or a counselor is also a way of caring for the life God gave you.
