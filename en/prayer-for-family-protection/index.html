Prayer for Family Protection — Cover Your Loved Ones with God's Shield Today

Place your family under God's divine protection right now. Pray this powerful prayer every morning and watch how God guards those you love most.

Text of the prayer

If you love your family and want God's hand of protection over them, this prayer is for you. Say it every morning and watch how God guards those you [music] love most. God said, "I will be a wall of fire around you and I will be the glory within you.

Your family is not forgotten. Heaven is watching over your home right now. Pray with me. Lord Jesus, I place my family under your protection today. Cover my spouse, my children, and every loved one with your blood and your peace. Guard our home from every danger.

Send your angels to surround us. Fill our hearts with your love. May nothing the enemy plans against us succeed. In your name we pray. Amen. Your prayer was heard and God received it with love. Your family is covered by his grace and protection. Pray [music] this every single morning and trust the Lord to be your family's ultimate protector, provider, and guardian.
