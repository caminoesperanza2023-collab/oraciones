Prayer for My Children: Pray With Me for Their Protection, Faith and Future

PRAYER FOR MY CHILDREN, prayed together: say the name of each of your children and pray with me, slowly and with no hurry, for about ten minutes. For worried mothers and fathers, to pray every night or on any day a child keeps you awake.

We pray Psalm 121, The Lord Is Your Keeper, the Our Father, the Hail Mary, the Sub Tuum Praesidium and the Hail Holy Queen, with five intentions: for their protection, for their faith, for their health and peace, for their studies, work and decisions, and for the children who are far away. We end with the parent's blessing from the Book of Numbers. You do not need to know the prayers by heart: I will lead and you simply pray along in your heart.

Full text of the prayer

Pray with me for your children

There is one prayer no mother or father ever stops praying, even without words: the prayer for their children. Think now of each one of them. Little or grown, living with you or on the other side of the world, close to God or far from him. Say their names softly, one by one. In the next few minutes we are going to place them together in the hands of God, slowly and with no hurry. You do not need to know how. Just stay here and pray with me. What you cannot do for them, God can.

Why we pray for our children

The Bible is full of mothers and fathers who prayed for their children. Job rose early in the morning to offer a sacrifice to God for each one of his. Hannah gave her son Samuel to the Lord. And Saint Monica prayed and wept for years for her son Augustine, who lived far from God, until she saw him come back. Praying for a child is not asking God to do everything our way: our children are free, and God respects that freedom. But no prayer of a mother or a father is ever lost. God loves your children even more than you do. Let us begin.

Opening prayer

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Lord Jesus, today I bring you my children. You knew them before I ever held them in my arms, and you know them better than I do. I give them to you with everything they are: their joys and their wounds, their dreams and their fears, what they tell me and what they keep to themselves. I also give you my mistakes as a father, as a mother. What I did not know how to give them, give it to them yourself. And watch over them where I cannot reach.

Psalm one hundred twenty-one: the Lord is your keeper

Let us now pray the psalm of protection, thinking of them. I lift up my eyes to the hills. From where does my help come? My help comes from the Lord, who made heaven and earth. He will not let your foot slip; he who keeps you will not slumber. The Lord is your keeper; the Lord is your shade at your right hand. The sun shall not strike you by day, nor the moon by night. The Lord will keep you from all evil; he will keep your life. The Lord will keep your going out and your coming in, from this time forth and for evermore. Lord, keep my children like this: when they leave home and when they come back, by day and by night, when they are well and when they suffer.

Our Father and Hail Mary

Let us pray together the prayers you may have taught them yourself. Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come, thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

For their protection

For their protection. Lord, I pray for my children on every road they travel. Protect them on the street, on the highway, at work, and in every place where I cannot see them. Keep them from accidents, from violence and from bad company. May their guardian angel never leave their side, not even for a moment. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my children and keep them in your love.

For their faith

For their faith. Lord, you know the heart of each one of my children. If one of them has drifted away from you, if they no longer pray or say they do not believe, never stop looking for them. You are the good shepherd who leaves the ninety-nine sheep to go after the one that was lost. Give me patience not to push them and faith not to give up. Saint Monica, you who prayed so many years for your son, pray with me for mine. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my children and keep them in your love.

For their health and peace

For their health and peace. Lord, I pray for the health of my children, in body and in soul. If one of them is sick, hold them up and heal whatever you choose to heal. If one of them lives with anxiety, sadness or fear, give them your peace and put someone good at their side. If one of them is fighting an addiction, give them the strength to ask for help. And teach me to listen to them without judging them. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my children and keep them in your love.

For their studies, work and decisions

For their studies, their work and their decisions. Lord, give my children light when they have to choose. Help them in their studies, and give them honest work that they do with integrity. Give them wisdom to choose their friends, and the person they will share their life with. May they seek not only success, but what is good. And if they make mistakes, may they know they can always come home. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my children and keep them in your love.

For the children who are far away

For the children who are far away. Lord, I pray for my children who live in another city or another country, and for those who are far away even when they live nearby. If there is a quarrel, a silence or a wound between us, heal it. If I have hurt them, give me the humility to ask their forgiveness. If they have hurt me, give me a heart that can forgive. May no pride ever be stronger than the love that binds us. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. Lord, watch over my children and keep them in your love.

A parent's blessing

Now, if you can, stretch out your hand toward them, even if they are not here, and bless them with the words God gave Moses to bless his people. The Lord bless you and keep you. The Lord make his face shine upon you and be gracious to you. The Lord lift up his countenance upon you and give you peace. My son, my daughter, I bless you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. This blessing is not magic: it is a father, a mother, placing their child under the gaze of God.

Under Mary's mantle

Let us now place our children under the mantle of the Virgin Mary, who was also a mother and also suffered for her Son. We fly to thy protection, O holy Mother of God. Despise not our petitions in our necessities, but deliver us always from all dangers, O glorious and blessed Virgin. Mother, you who searched for Jesus for three days when he stayed behind in Jerusalem, search for my children when they are lost. Saint Joseph, protector of the Holy Family, watch over mine as well. Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen.

My children are yours

Lord, I end this prayer, but I do not stop trusting. My children are yours before they are mine. Lord, watch over my children and keep them in your love. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. We will pray for them, and other parents who pray here will do the same.
