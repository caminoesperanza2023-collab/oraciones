Bedtime Prayer: Lord, Give Me Peaceful Sleep Tonight

Bedtime prayer: if you cannot sleep or your mind will not stop worrying, pray this short Catholic night prayer with me and give God what keeps you awake.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Psalm 4 says: in peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, Lord, make me dwell in safety.

Lord, this day is ending and I give it to you just as it was. Forgive what I did wrong and keep what was good. Quiet my thoughts, which will not stop turning. I leave you tomorrow's worries: you do not sleep, you watch over me. Guard this house while we sleep, and every person I love. Give me peaceful rest and wake me with new strength.

Mary, my Mother, cover me with your mantle tonight.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, O Lord, make me dwell in safety" (Psalm 4:8). The Church ends every day with Night Prayer: we give God the day as it was, ask forgiveness, leave tomorrow in his hands and ask Mary to cover us with her mantle.

What the Church teaches

The Church ends each day with Compline, or Night Prayer, which includes an examination of conscience and the words of Psalm 4 and Psalm 91. Giving God the day before sleeping is an ancient Christian habit: it does not cure every sleepless night, but it teaches the heart to rest in trust.
