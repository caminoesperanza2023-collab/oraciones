Saint Faustina: The Mystic Who Gave the World Divine Mercy

Helena Kowalska — Saint Faustina — was the tenth of twelve children of a poor Polish family. She entered a convent as a lay sister to do the cleaning. And Jesus chose her to deliver one of the most powerful messages of the 20th century.

Her diary has over 600 pages of theological depth — written by a nun with minimal education.

What the video says

Helena Kowalsska was born in Poland in 1905, the 10th of 12 children in a very poor family. She never went to university. She entered a convent as a lay sister essentially to do cleaning and cooking. And yet Jesus chose her to deliver to the entire world one of the most powerful messages of the 20th century.

Jesus, I trust in you. In her diary, Fainino wrote over 600 pages of visions, conversations with Jesus, and spiritual teachings, all with minimal education. The theologians who have studied them are astonished by their depth. The central message is simple and devastating. God is infinitely merciful, more merciful than you can imagine.

No sin is too great for his forgiveness if you repent. St. Fina was canonized by John Paul II in the year 2000. Her image Jesus with two rays of light is in millions of homes around the world. The message she carried is for you today.
