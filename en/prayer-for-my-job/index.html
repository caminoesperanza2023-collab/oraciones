Prayer for My Job: Lord, Bless the Work of My Hands

Prayer for my job: if you are looking for work, or the job you have weighs you down and steals your peace, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and place your work and your family's daily bread in the hands of Jesus.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Jesus said: come to me, all you who labor and are burdened, and I will give you rest.

Lord, today I place in your hands my work and my family's daily bread. Saint Joseph, worker of Nazareth, pray for me. Open the doors I need, give me strength for every day, and take from my heart the anxiety about tomorrow.

May my work be honest, may I do it with love, and may we never lack what we need. You feed the birds of the sky; I trust in you.

Amen.

What the Bible says

"Come to me, all you who labor and are burdened, and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28). Ask Saint Joseph, the carpenter of Nazareth, to intercede for you, so the right doors open and you never lack what you need.

What the Church teaches

Saint Joseph earned his living as a carpenter in Nazareth, and Jesus learned his trade: "Is he not the carpenter?" (Mark 6:3). That is why the Church celebrates Saint Joseph the Worker every May 1. The Catechism teaches that work, united to Christ, can be a path to holiness (CCC 2427). And Psalm 90:17 prays: "Prosper the work of our hands."
