Prayer for a Child Who Left the Faith: Lord, Bring Them Home

Prayer for a child who left the faith: if your son or daughter has walked away from God or the Church, pray this short Catholic prayer with me and do not lose hope.

Pray along with the video

Text of the prayer

Saint Monica prayed with tears for many years, and her son became Saint Augustine.

Lord Jesus, you leave the ninety-nine to look for the one who is lost. Today I give you my child. Go after them wherever they are. Soften their heart, send good people into their path, and bring them back to your house. And give me patience, so that my love speaks louder than my words.

Saint Monica, pray for us. Virgin Mary, mother of all who wander, bring my child home.

Amen.

What the Bible says

Jesus leaves the ninety-nine to find the one that is lost (Luke 15:4-7), and the father of the prodigal son runs to embrace him (Luke 15:20). Saint Monica prayed for years for her son, who became Saint Augustine.

What the Church teaches

Saint Monica prayed and wept for her son Augustine for many years; a bishop told her, "the child of those tears shall never perish." Augustine was baptized in 387 and became a Doctor of the Church (Confessions, Book III and IX). Jesus describes God as the shepherd who seeks the lost sheep and the father who runs to the returning son (Luke 15). Conversion is God's work, and our prayer and patient love open the door (Catechism 1432).
